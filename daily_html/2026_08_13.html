StellaVLA: In-Context Structured Demonstration for Generalizable Vision-Language-Action Models VLA

In-context structured demonstration Vision-Language-Action test-time adaptation out-of-distribution generalization dual-training design

简要说明：StellaVLA 提出了一种框架，通过在测试时使用单个检索到的结构化演示来适应视觉-语言-动作模型，该演示传达任务推理而非模仿，从而无需微调即可泛化到分布外任务。

TLDR: StellaVLA introduces a framework that adapts Vision-Language-Action models at test time using a single retrieved structured demonstration, which conveys task reasoning rather than imitation, enabling generalization to out-of-distribution tasks without fine-tuning.

Abstract: Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models can follow instructions and manipulate objects, but their performance often collapses out of distribution (OOD), when the scene, viewpoint, or object differs from training. Adapting to each new situation typically requires collecting more data and fine-tuning. We present StellaVLA, a framework that instead adapts at test time by conditioning on a single retrieved demonstration. The key idea is to move beyond imitating what an expert did and instead convey why: an automated offline pipeline converts each raw trajectory into a structured demonstration, e.g., a task plan, sub-goal descriptions, and verbalized 3D motion, at zero human-annotation cost. Provided as in-context guidance, this structured demonstration lets the policy reason about the task rather than mimic a pixel trajectory, which also makes it transferable across embodiments (real-robot, human-hand, or XR demonstrations). A parallel dual-training design internalizes this reasoning during training through a joint action-and-language objective, while inference uses the action expert alone, preserving real-time, high-frequency control with no added latency. On the VLA-Arena leaderboard(Aug 1, 2026), StellaVLA ranks first with an overall score of 0.63, versus 0.44 and 0.22 for the strong prior models ($π_{0.5}$ and LingBot-VLA), and it further leads on LIBERO with 98.8% average success rate and LIBERO-Plus with 85.1% success rate. Our real-robot benchmark demonstrates that StellaVLA can use both human/robot demos and human-to-robot (XR) demos as in-context structured demonstration to help VLA model adapt to OOD tasks.

G0.5: One Autoregressive Stream for Robot Reasoning and Action VLA

autoregressive VLA action tokenizer chain-of-thought visual memory cross-embodiment

简要说明：G0.5 是一种预训练的自回归 VLA 模型，通过可学习的跨本体动作分词器、思维链交错和视觉记忆模块，将推理和动作统一在单个 Transformer 解码器中，在多种机器人操作基准上取得了最先进的结果。

TLDR: G0.5 is a pretrained autoregressive VLA model that unifies reasoning and action in a single transformer decoder via a learnable cross-embodiment action tokenizer, chain-of-thought interleaving, and visual memory, achieving state-of-the-art results across diverse robot manipulation benchmarks.

Abstract: The prevailing recipe for Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models couples a pretrained VLM with a separately trained flow-matching action expert. This makes the VLM a context encoder rather than a decision-maker. We introduce G0.5, a pretrained autoregressive VLA in which a single transformer decoder emits reasoning and action tokens under a single objective. Three components make this tractable at foundation-model scale: a learnable cross-embodiment action tokenizer that maps heterogeneous robot actions into a shared vocabulary; a native chain-of-thought stream interleaving task decomposition, object grounding, and action hints with action tokens; and a visual memory module that injects multi-second history through the vision encoder. Because reasoning and action share a single set of weights, the pretrained VLM's capabilities carry over to physical behavior: the model follows instructions closely, and prompts directly steer action granularity, task horizon, and out-of-distribution scene handling without further training. Pretrained on a large collection of robot datasets together with VQA samples, G0.5 surpasses state-of-the-art models across 7 independent regimes: real-world fine-tuning on R1lite and R1pro robots (76.7\% vs.\ 53.3\% for $π_{0.5}$ and 24.4\% for GR00T-N1.7), the 2025 BEHAVIOR Challenge on 50 long-horizon household mobile manipulation tasks using a generalist policy (31.4\% vs.\ 26.3\% for $π_{0.5}$ and 26.1\% for the challenge winner), DROID post-training followed by zero-shot transfer to an unseen environment and objects (82.5\%), a language-following Pick-and-Place benchmark, LIBERO (98.9\%), RoboTwin 2.0 (93.3\%), and SimplerEnv-Bridge (87.3\%).

摘要（中文）：主流的视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型构建方案是将预训练的VLM与一个单独训练的流匹配动作专家模块耦合。这使得VLM仅充当上下文编码器而非决策者。我们提出G0.5，一个预训练的自回归VLA模型，其中单个Transformer解码器在单一目标下同时输出推理和动作令牌。三个组件使其在基础模型规模下可行：一个可学习的跨本体动作分词器，将异构机器人动作映射到共享词汇表；一个原生思维链流，将任务分解、目标定位和动作提示与动作令牌交错；以及一个视觉记忆模块，通过视觉编码器注入多秒历史信息。由于推理和动作共享同一套权重，预训练VLM的能力可直接迁移到物理行为：模型严格遵循指令，且无需额外训练即可通过提示直接控制动作粒度、任务范围及分布外场景处理。G0.5在大规模机器人数据集与VQA样本上预训练后，在7个独立场景中均超越现有最佳模型：在R1lite和R1pro机器人上的真实世界微调（76.7\%对比$π_{0.5}$的53.3\%和GR00T-N1.7的24.4\%），2025年BEHAVIOR挑战中使用通用策略完成50项长时域家庭移动操作任务（31.4\%对比$π_{0.5}$的26.3\%和挑战冠军的26.1\%），DROID后训练后在未见环境与物体上零样本迁移（82.5\%），语言引导的抓取放置基准LIBERO（98.9\%），RoboTwin 2.0（93.3\%），以及SimplerEnv-Bridge（87.3\%）。

RoadWeaver: Large-Scale Lane-Level HD Map Generation from Scratch for Autonomous Driving Simulation AutonomousDriving

HD map generation autonomous driving simulation coarse-to-fine lane-level geometry closed-loop evaluation

简要说明：RoadWeaver是一个从粗到细的框架，能够从零生成多样化、大规模的车道级高清地图，用于自动驾驶模拟，具有高可达性和低死胡同率，可直接部署在驾驶模拟器中。

TLDR: RoadWeaver is a coarse-to-fine framework that generates diverse, large-scale lane-level HD maps from scratch for autonomous driving simulation, achieving high reachability and low dead-end ratio, enabling direct deployment in driving simulators.

Abstract: Autonomous driving simulation requires diverse and scalable lane-level HD maps to support long-horizon evaluation across complex road networks. Existing approaches either rely on handcrafted or reconstructed real-world maps, which limits scalability, or generate only local road structures rather than complete HD maps. We present RoadWeaver, a coarse-to-fine framework for from-scratch generation of diverse, large-scale HD maps. RoadWeaver first synthesizes a global road layout, expands it into a connected road network, and then constructs lane-level geometry with topologically consistent lane connectivity. Experimental results show that RoadWeaver achieves a 99.8\% reachability, a 10.7\% dead-end ratio, and an endpoint alignment error of 0.24 m. Compared with SOTA generation methods, it reduces endpoint alignment error by 94.4\% while generating complete HD maps in 1.39--3.50 s. The generated maps can be directly deployed in driving simulators, providing scalable simulation environments for future closed-loop evaluation of autonomous driving systems. The training code and an out-of-the-box implementation of RoadWeaver will be released upon acceptance.

摘要（中文）：自动驾驶仿真需要多样化且可扩展的车道级高清地图，以支持复杂路网上的长时域评估。现有方法要么依赖人工制作或重建的真实地图（这限制了可扩展性），要么仅生成局部道路结构而非完整高清地图。我们提出RoadWeaver，一种从粗到细的框架，用于从零生成多样化、大规模的高清地图。RoadWeaver首先合成全局道路布局，将其扩展为连通路网，随后构建具有拓扑一致车道连接性的车道级几何结构。实验结果表明，RoadWeaver实现了99.8%的可达性、10.7%的断头路比例以及0.24米的端点对齐误差。与现有最优生成方法相比，它在1.39-3.50秒内生成完整高清地图的同时，将端点对齐误差降低了94.4%。生成的地图可直接部署于驾驶仿真器，为未来自动驾驶系统的闭环评估提供可扩展的仿真环境。RoadWeaver的训练代码与开箱即用实现将在论文接收后发布。

Keep the Future, Drop the Rollout: RIFT for World Action Models VLA

world action model rollout-free future conditioning key/value cache action-chunk latency LIBERO

简要说明：RIFT 提出了一种无需视频展开的未来条件化方法，通过单次骨干网络前向传播构建完整的未来键值缓存，在保持与展开方法相近成功率的同时，将动作块延迟降低 68-89%。

TLDR: RIFT proposes a rollout-free future-conditioning method for world action models that constructs a complete future key/value cache in one backbone pass, achieving comparable success rates to rollout-based methods while reducing action-chunk latency by 68–89%.

Abstract: World action models (WAMs) condition robot actions on predicted futures, but iterative video rollout increases deployment latency. We ask whether action generation requires the evolving rollout trajectory or only its future representation. Across four WAMs on all 40 LIBERO tasks, paired closed-loop interventions show that masking or reassigning future-cache values changes execution and reduces success, indicating sensitivity to future values and their assigned positions. For Joint and Cosmos-2, however, replaying one fixed final-clean key/value (K/V) cache nearly preserves unmodified execution, with $1.7$ to $1.9$~cm end-effector average displacement error and $97.9\%$ to $98.2\%$ success. This separates cache consumption from production: these models can reuse a fixed cache but still require iterative rollout to construct it. We therefore propose RIFT (\emph{Rollout-free Imagination via Future Tokens}), which uses learned anticipation tokens to construct a complete future K/V cache in one backbone pass while retaining the original future-read interface. On LIBERO, RIFT achieves $98.8\%$ success, close to rollout-based Joint, IDM, and LingBot-VA at $98.4\%$ to $98.6\%$, while reducing action-chunk latency by $68.2\%$ to $89.1\%$. On RoboTwin~2.0, RIFT reaches $92.9/92.6\%$ on clean/randomized scenes, the highest observed among the evaluated methods. These results support rollout-free future conditioning without iterative video generation at deployment.

摘要（中文）：世界动作模型（WAMs）通过预测未来状态来约束机器人动作，但迭代式视频推演会增加部署延迟。我们探究动作生成是否需要完整的推演轨迹，抑或仅需其未来表征即可。针对所有40个LIBERO任务中的四种WAM，成对闭环干预实验表明：遮蔽或重新分配未来缓存（future-cache）数值会改变执行过程并降低成功率，这表明模型对未来数值及其分配位置具有敏感性。然而，对于Joint和Cosmos-2模型，重放单一固定的最终清洁键/值（K/V）缓存几乎能保持原始执行效果，末端执行器平均位移误差为1.7至1.9厘米，成功率达97.9%至98.2%。这区分了缓存消耗与缓存生成过程：这些模型虽能复用固定缓存，但构建缓存仍需迭代推演。为此我们提出RIFT（基于未来令牌的无推演想象），该方法通过习得的预期令牌，在单次骨干网络前向传播中构建完整未来K/V缓存，同时保留原始的未来读取接口。在LIBERO上，RIFT实现98.8%成功率，接近基于推演的Joint、IDM和LingBot-VA（98.4%至98.6%），同时将动作片段延迟降低68.2%至89.1%。在RoboTwin 2.0上，RIFT在清洁/随机场景中分别达到92.9%和92.6%的成功率，为评估方法中最高观测值。这些结果支持部署时无需迭代视频生成的无推演未来条件约束方案。

Abstract: Integrating locomotion and manipulation is essential for robot autonomy, but scaling standard Reinforcement Learning (RL) to complex tasks is severely bottlenecked by the slow, manual process of dense reward shaping. To bypass this limitation, we leverage Sample-based Model Predictive Control (SMPC) entirely in simulation as an automated, rapidly tunable expert to generate massive offline datasets. Because this data solves the fundamental exploration problem, we can train an off-policy RL agent using purely sparse task rewards, drastically reducing the time required to learn new skills and eliminating the need for manual tuning. Integrating this high-level agent with a low-level dynamic stability controller yields more optimal behaviors that strictly align with true task objectives, ultimately allowing the learned policies to surpass the original optimal control teacher. Policy-Induced Hand Priors in Humanoid Dual-Arm Manipulation: Diagnosing and Mitigating Initial-Pose Dependence HumanoidEmbodied

简要说明：本论文在基于VLA的人形双臂操作中识别并量化了初始姿态依赖性，揭示了策略诱导的手部先验，该先验随配置和策略变化，并证明了针对性数据增强可提高鲁棒性。

TLDR: This paper identifies and quantifies initial-pose dependence in VLA-based humanoid dual-arm manipulation, revealing a policy-induced hand prior that varies across configurations and policies, and demonstrates that targeted data augmentation improves robustness.

Abstract: Vision-language-action (VLA) policies are expected to operate robustly across variations in the robot's initial configuration, yet aggregate task success can conceal pose-specific failures and inappropriate hand selection. This work investigates initial-pose dependence in VLA-based humanoid dual-arm manipulation. We characterize the initial-condition-dependent early hand preference as a policy-induced hand prior and quantify it using HandPriorScore, residual hand bias, and target responsiveness. Evaluations across multiple policies and 17 initial configurations reveal strong initial-pose--policy interactions: the same pose produces substantially different success rates across policies, while a single policy exhibits large performance variation across poses. Specific initial arm configurations can suppress or induce an asymmetric hand preference, with the resulting effect varying in direction and strength across policies. Wrist-camera observations also influence hand selection and task performance. Expanding initial-pose coverage in the training dataset substantially improves robustness, while targeted augmentation around a low-performing configuration increases its success rate.
