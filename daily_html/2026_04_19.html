Chain Of Interaction Benchmark (COIN): When Reasoning meets Embodied Interaction

简要说明：为评估机器人操控中的交互推理能力，提出了一个新基准，包含50个具有因果依赖关系的任务数据集，并建立了评估现有VLA和策略方法的指标。

TLDR: A new benchmark for evaluating interactive reasoning in robotic manipulation, featuring a dataset of 50 tasks with causally-dependent interactions and metrics to assess current VLA and policy methods.

Abstract: Generalist embodied agents must perform interactive, causally-dependent reasoning, continually interacting with the environment, acquiring information, and updating plans to solve long-horizon tasks before they could be adopted in real-life scenarios. For instance, retrieving an apple from a cabinet may require opening multiple doors and drawers before the apple becomes visible and reachable, demanding sequential interaction under partial observability. However, existing benchmarks fail to systematically evaluate this essential capability. We introduce COIN, a benchmark designed to assess interactive reasoning in realistic robotic manipulation through three key contributions. First, we construct COIN-50: 50 interactive tasks in daily scenarios, and create COIN-Primitive required by causally-dependent tasks, and COIN-Composition with mid-term complexity for skill learning and generalization evaluation. Second, we develop a low-cost mobile AR teleoperation system and collect the COIN-Primitive Dataset with 50 demonstrations per primitive task (1,000 in total). Third, we develop systematic evaluation metrics about execution stability and generalization robustness to evaluate CodeAsPolicy, VLA, and language-conditioned H-VLA approaches. Our comprehensive evaluation reveals critical limitations in current methods: models struggle with interactive reasoning tasks due to significant gaps between visual understanding and motor execution. We provide fine-grained analysis of these limitations.

摘要（中文）：通用型具身智能体必须执行交互式、因果依赖的推理，通过持续与环境互动、获取信息并更新规划来解决长期任务，方能在现实场景中得到应用。例如，从橱柜中取出苹果可能需要先打开多扇门和抽屉，待苹果可见且可触及后才能完成操作，这要求智能体在部分可观测条件下进行序列化交互。然而，现有基准测试未能系统评估这一核心能力。我们提出COIN基准，通过三大关键贡献评估现实机器人操作中的交互推理能力：首先，构建了日常场景下的50项交互任务COIN-50，创建了因果依赖任务所需的COIN-Primitive基础模块，以及用于技能学习与泛化评估的中等复杂度组合任务COIN-Composition。其次，开发了低成本移动增强现实遥操作系统，并收集了COIN-Primitive数据集（每个基础任务包含50次演示，总计1000次）。第三，建立了涵盖执行稳定性与泛化鲁棒性的系统评估指标，用以评测CodeAsPolicy、视觉语言模型及语言条件化混合视觉语言方法。综合评估揭示了当前方法的显著局限：由于视觉理解与动作执行间的巨大鸿沟，模型在交互推理任务中表现挣扎。我们针对这些局限提供了细粒度分析。

LongBench: Evaluating Robotic Manipulation Policies on Real-World Long-Horizon Tasks

简要说明：该论文提出了LongBench，一个包含超过1000个真实世界回合的基准，用于评估长视距机器人操作策略，旨在诊断与执行鲁棒性和上下文依赖推理相关的故障。

TLDR: The paper introduces LongBench, a real-world benchmark with over 1,000 episodes for evaluating long-horizon robotic manipulation policies, designed to diagnose failures related to execution robustness and context-dependent reasoning.

Abstract: Robotic manipulation policies often degrade over extended horizons, yet existing benchmarks provide limited insight into why such failures occur. Most prior benchmarks are either simulation-based or report aggregate success, making it difficult to disentangle the distinct sources of temporal difficulty in real-world execution. We introduce LongBench, a real-world benchmark for evaluating long-horizon manipulation. LongBench consists of over 1,000 real-world episodes, covering two complementary regimes: Context-Independent (fully observable) and Context-Dependent (ambiguity-driven). By organizing tasks into capability- and ambiguity-specific subsets, LongBench enables mechanism-aware evaluation of execution robustness, temporal consistency, and context-dependent reasoning. Evaluating six state-of-the-art policies reveals that long-horizon performance is not governed by a single factor. We observe that performance in fully observable settings is more strongly associated with execution robustness, while contextual difficulty varies across tasks and is not consistently improved by memory-based methods. We hope that LongBench serves as a useful benchmark for studying long-horizon manipulation and for developing policies with stronger robustness across both execution and contextual challenges.

摘要（中文）：机器人操作策略在长时间任务中性能往往会出现衰减，然而现有基准测试对其失效原因的分析能力有限。大多数先前基准测试要么基于仿真环境，要么仅报告聚合成功率，难以区分实际执行中不同类型的时间性困难。我们提出LongBench——一个用于评估长时程操作的真实世界基准测试框架。LongBench包含超过1000组真实环境操作序列，涵盖两种互补机制：情境无关（完全可观测）和情境依赖（模糊性驱动）。通过将任务按能力需求和模糊特性进行分类组织，LongBench实现了对执行鲁棒性、时序一致性及情境依赖推理能力的机制化评估。对六种前沿策略的测试表明，长时程表现并非由单一因素决定。我们观察到在完全可观测场景下的性能与执行鲁棒性关联更强，而情境性困难则随任务变化存在差异，基于记忆的方法并未能持续改善此类问题。我们希望LongBench能成为研究长时程操作、开发兼具执行鲁棒性与情境适应能力策略的有效基准测试平台。

ReconVLA: An Uncertainty-Guided and Failure-Aware Vision-Language-Action Framework for Robotic Control

简要说明：本文提出了ReconVLA框架，利用共形预测为用于机器人控制的视觉-语言-动作模型提供校准的不确定性估计和故障检测机制。

TLDR: This paper introduces ReconVLA, a framework that uses conformal prediction to provide calibrated uncertainty estimates and failure detection for Vision-Language-Action models in robotic control tasks.

Abstract: Vision-language-action (VLA) models have emerged as generalist robotic controllers capable of mapping visual observations and natural language instructions to continuous action sequences. However, VLAs provide no calibrated measure of confidence in their action predictions, thus limiting their reliability in real-world settings where uncertainty and failures must be anticipated. To address this problem we introduce ReconVLA, a reliable conformal model that produces uncertainty-guided and failure-aware control signals. Concretely, our approach applies conformal prediction directly to the action token outputs of pretrained VLA policies, yielding calibrated uncertainty estimates that correlate with execution quality and task success. Furthermore, we extend conformal prediction to the robot state space to detect outliers or unsafe states before failures occur, providing a simple yet effective failure detection mechanism that complements the action-level uncertainty. We evaluate ReconVLA in both simulation and real robot experiments across diverse manipulation tasks. Our results show that conformalized action predictions consistently improve failure anticipation, reduce catastrophic errors, and provide a calibrated measure of confidence without retraining or modifying the underlying VLA.

摘要（中文）：视觉-语言-行动（VLA）模型已成为一种通用机器人控制器，能够将视觉观测与自然语言指令映射为连续动作序列。然而，VLA模型无法对其动作预测提供经过校准的置信度度量，这限制了其在现实场景中的可靠性——这些场景中必须预见到不确定性和失败风险。为解决这一问题，我们提出了ReconVLA：一种可靠的共形模型，可生成不确定性引导且具备故障感知能力的控制信号。具体而言，该方法将共形预测直接应用于预训练VLA策略输出的动作标记，从而产生与执行质量及任务成功率相关的校准化不确定性估计。此外，我们将共形预测扩展至机器人状态空间，以在故障发生前检测异常或不安全状态，形成一种简单有效的故障检测机制，与动作层面的不确定性分析形成互补。我们在模拟和真实机器人实验中通过多样化操作任务对ReconVLA进行评估。结果表明，经过共形化的动作预测能持续提升故障预判能力，减少灾难性错误，并在无需重新训练或修改底层VLA模型的前提下提供校准化的置信度度量。

Neural Network-Based Adaptive Event-Triggered Control for Dual-Arm Unmanned Aerial Manipulator Systems

简要说明：针对双机械臂空中操作器系统，提出一种基于神经网络的、具有自适应事件触发机制的控制方案，以解决系统耦合、未建模动态和通信限制，实现稳定的轨迹跟踪。

TLDR: Proposes an adaptive event-triggered neural network control scheme for a dual-arm aerial manipulator, addressing coupling, disturbances, and communication constraints to achieve stable trajectory tracking.

Abstract: This paper investigates the control problem of dual-arm unmanned aerial manipulator systems (DAUAMs). Strong coupling between the dual-arm and the multirotor platform, together with unmodeled dynamics and external disturbances, poses significant challenges to stable and accurate operation. An adaptive event-triggered control scheme with neural network-based approximation is proposed to address these issues while explicitly considering communication constraints. First, a dynamic model of the DAUAM system is derived, and a command-filter-based backstepping framework with error compensation is constructed. Then, a neural network is employed to approximate external frictions, and an event-triggered mechanism is designed to reduce the transmission frequency of control updates, thereby alleviating communication and energy burdens. Lyapunov-based analysis shows that all closed-loop signals remain bounded and that the tracking error converges to a neighborhood of the desired trajectory within a fixed time. Finally, experiments on a self-built DAUAM platform demonstrate that the proposed approach achieves accurate trajectory tracking.

Rule-VLN: Bridging Perception and Compliance via Semantic Reasoning and Geometric Rectification

简要说明：提出了Rule-VLN基准，用于测试智能体在导航中遵守社会与语义规则的能力，并提出了SNRM模块，为零样本增强现有智能体的安全合规意识。

TLDR: Introduces Rule-VLN, a benchmark for rule-compliant navigation requiring agents to obey social and semantic constraints, along with a zero-shot module (SNRM) to equip existing agents with safety awareness.

Abstract: As embodied AI transitions to real-world deployment, the success of the Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) task tends to evolve from mere reachability to social compliance. However, current agents suffer from a "goal-driven trap", prioritizing physical geometry ("can I go?") over semantic rules ("may I go?"), frequently overlooking subtle regulatory constraints. To bridge this gap, we establish Rule-VLN, the first large-scale urban benchmark for rule-compliant navigation. Spanning a massive 29k-node environment, it injects 177 diverse regulatory categories into 8k constrained nodes across four curriculum levels, challenging agents with fine-grained visual and behavioral constraints. We further propose the Semantic Navigation Rectification Module (SNRM), a universal, zero-shot module designed to equip pre-trained agents with safety awareness. SNRM integrates a coarse-to-fine visual perception VLM framework with an epistemic mental map for dynamic detour planning. Experiments demonstrate that while Rule-VLN challenges state-of-the-art models, SNRM significantly restores navigation capabilities, reducing CVR by 19.26% and boosting TC by 5.97%.

TLDR: Proposes a VR-based gamified framework using a humanoid robot in Unity to collect scalable, diverse training data for embodied intelligence tasks, validated with a pick-and-place task.

Abstract: Collecting embodied interaction data at scale remains costly and difficult due to the limited accessibility of conventional interfaces. We present a gamified data collection framework based on Unity that combines procedural scene generation, VR-based humanoid robot control, automatic task evaluation, and trajectory logging. A trash pick-and-place task prototype is developed to validate the full workflow.Experimental results indicate that the collected demonstrations exhibit broad coverage of the state-action space, and that increasing task difficulty leads to higher motion intensity as well as more extensive exploration of the arm's workspace. Time-Division Multiplexing Actuation in Tendon-Driven Arms: Lightweight Design and Fault Tolerance

简要说明：本文提出了一种用于肌腱驱动机械臂的时分复用驱动方法，通过新型硬件设计和轨迹规划算法，实现了轻量化、高负载且具备容错能力的机器人系统。

TLDR: This paper introduces Time-Division Multiplexing Actuation, a hardware design and control method for lightweight, fault-tolerant tendon-driven robotic arms, featuring a new actuator mechanism and trajectory planning algorithm.

Abstract: Robotic manipulators for aerospace applications require a delicate balance between lightweight construction and fault-tolerant operation to satisfy strict weight limitations and ensure reliability in remote, hazardous environments. This paper presents Time-Division Multiplexing Actuation (TDMA), a practical approach for tendon-driven robots that significantly reduces actuator count while preserving high torque output and intrinsic fault tolerance. The key hardware employs a vertically-stacked rotational selection structure that integrates self-rotating TDM motors for rapid configuration, electromagnetic clutches enabling sub-0.1 second engagement, a worm gear reducer for enhanced load capacity and self-locking capability, and a dual-encoder system for precise, long-term positioning. Leveraging TDMA, the proposed MuxArm achieves a self-weight of 2.17 kg, supports an actuator driving capacity of 10 kg, and maintains end-effector accuracy up to 1% of its length, even under partial servo failure. Additionally, an actuation space trajectory planning algorithm is developed, enabling fault-tolerant control and reducing tendon load by up to 50% compared to conventional methods. Comprehensive experiments demonstrate MuxArm's robust performance in diverse settings, including free-space, cluttered, and confined environments.

Refinement of Accelerated Demonstrations via Incremental Iterative Reference Learning Control for Fast Contact-Rich Imitation Learning

简要说明：提出一种方法，通过逐步增加速度和更新参考轨迹，为接触密集模仿学习中的机器人演示进行时间加速优化，并在擦白板和孔插钉等真实任务上进行了验证。

TLDR: Proposes a method to refine time-accelerated robot demonstrations for imitation learning in contact-rich manipulation by incrementally increasing speed and updating the reference trajectory, validated on real-world tasks like whiteboard erasing and peg-in-hole.

Abstract: Fast execution of contact-rich manipulation is critical for practical deployment, yet providing fast demonstrations for imitation learning (IL) remains challenging: humans cannot demonstrate at high speed, and naively accelerating demonstrations alters contact dynamics and induces large tracking errors. We present a method to autonomously refine time-accelerated demonstrations by repurposing Iterative Reference Learning Control (IRLC) to iteratively update the reference trajectory from observed tracking errors. However, applying IRLC directly at high speed tends to produce larger early-iteration errors and less stable transients. To address this issue, we propose Incremental Iterative Reference Learning Control (I2RLC), which gradually increases the speed while updating the reference, yielding high-fidelity trajectories. We validate on real-robot whiteboard erasing and peg-in-hole tasks using a teleoperation setup with a compliance-controlled follower and a 3D-printed haptic leader. Both IRLC and I2RLC achieve up to 10x faster demonstrations with reduced tracking error; moreover, I2RLC improves spatial similarity to the original trajectories by 22.5% on average over IRLC across three tasks and multiple speeds (3x-10x). We then use the refined trajectories to train IL policies; the resulting policies execute faster than the demonstrations and achieve 100% success rates in the peg-in-hole task at both seen and unseen positions, with I2RLC-trained policies exhibiting lower contact forces than those trained on IRLC-refined demonstrations. These results indicate that gradual speed scheduling coupled with reference adaptation provides a practical path to fast, contact-rich IL.
