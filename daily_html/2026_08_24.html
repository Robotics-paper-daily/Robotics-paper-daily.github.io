EMPIRE: Explicit Manipulation Planning as a Learnable Intermediate Representation for Egocentric Hand-Motion Forecasting Manipulation

hand-motion forecasting manipulation planning egocentric perception bimanual intermediate representation

简要说明：EMPIRE 提出了一种两阶段框架，首先从多模态上下文中学习显式操作计划，然后利用这些冻结的计划来条件化运动生成器，以从自我中心视角预测双手运动，实现了最先进的预测精度。

TLDR: EMPIRE introduces a two-stage framework that first learns explicit manipulation plans from multimodal context, then uses these frozen plans to condition a motion generator for forecasting bimanual hand motions from egocentric views, achieving state-of-the-art accuracy.

Abstract: Forecasting dexterous hand motions from egocentric observations is fundamental to intelligent interactive systems. Existing VLM-based methods typically map observations directly to future motions, overlooking the underlying manipulation process that governs hand-object interactions. Moreover, end-to-end optimization couples manipulation learning with motion synthesis, causing motion-generation gradients to interfere with the pre-learned manipulation-aware representations. To overcome these limitations, we propose EMPIRE, a two-stage framework that introduces Explicit Manipulation Planning as an Intermediate Representation for Egocentric hand-motion forecasting. Stage I: Learn to Plan. EMPIRE first learns explicit manipulation plans from multimodal context to capture the progression of hand-object interactions. Stage II: Learn to Act. A motion generator synthesizes future bimanual hand motions conditioned on frozen planner representations, preventing motion-generation gradients from affecting manipulation planning. To support our method, we further construct EMPIRE-651K, a bimanual hand-motion forecasting dataset comprising 650,910 training windows across 111 tasks, each paired with an explicit per-hand manipulation plan. Under identical training and evaluation protocols, EMPIRE achieves state-of-the-art forecasting accuracy, with an MPJPE of 84.53 mm and a finger-relative error of 38.97mm. We release the code and dataset at https://github.com/wangwen-banban/EMPIRE.

Where World Models Break: Natural-Input Failure Discovery WorldModel

world models failure discovery prediction risk uncertainty-guided search typing-based replacement

简要说明：本文提出BasinLens方法，用于发现世界模型在罕见条件-动作组合下的灾难性预测失败，这些失败在平均评估中被忽略。

TLDR: This paper proposes BasinLens, a method to discover catastrophic prediction failures in world models by searching for rare condition-action combinations that cause severe errors, which are missed by average-case evaluations.

Abstract: World models predict action-conditioned futures and serve as critical internal simulators for downstream planning and control. However, catastrophic prediction failures of world models could dangerously propagate through the control pipeline, as subsequent agent or model training and decision-making depend heavily on the continuous environment evolution forecasted by these world models. Existing evaluations overlook this systemic risk: by aggregating average errors over benign generations from general queries, they fail to stress-test the model against catastrophic collapses under rare or unobserved condition-action combinations. To bridge this gap, we formalize the natural-input failure discovery problem: under a finite query budget, finding environment-valid conditions and action prefixes that induce severe prediction risk, verifying whether these failures reproduce on fresh seeds, and testing their persistence under nearby valid edits. Discovering such critical failures is computationally challenging, as valid condition-action combinations explode exponentially, rendering exhaustive search or standard sampling infeasible given the high cost of noisy rollouts. To tackle this, we propose BasinLens, which exploits the underlying structure of valid inputs, where each coordinate possesses environment-defined semantic types and admissible domains, by pairing uncertainty-guided global search with typed local replacements. Across diverse benchmarks and world-model families, BasinLens exposes reproducible and locally persistent failure modes that conventional evaluations fail to reveal, showing that average-case benchmarks can mask important vulnerabilities in world-model-driven control.

摘要（中文）：世界模型能够预测基于动作的未来状态，是下游规划与控制中至关重要的内部模拟器。然而，世界模型的灾难性预测失败可能会危及其在控制流程中的传播，因为后续的智能体或模型训练与决策在很大程度上依赖于这些世界模型所预测的连续环境演化。现有评估忽略了这一系统性风险：通过聚合一般查询中良性生成结果的平均误差，这些评估未能针对在罕见或未观测的条件-动作组合下可能出现的灾难性崩溃对模型进行压力测试。为弥合这一空白，我们正式定义了自然输入故障发现问题：在有限查询预算下，寻找能够诱发严重预测风险的、符合环境条件的条件与动作前缀，验证这些故障是否能在新的随机种子下复现，并测试它们在附近有效编辑条件下的持续性。发现此类关键故障在计算上极具挑战性，因为有效的条件-动作组合呈指数级增长，使得在噪声展开成本高昂的情况下，穷举搜索或标准采样不可行。为解决这一问题，我们提出了BasinLens，该方法通过将不确定性引导的全局搜索与类型化的局部替换相结合，利用了有效输入的底层结构——其中每个坐标都具备环境定义的语义类型与允许域。在多样化的基准测试与世界模型家族中，BasinLens揭示了传统评估无法发现的、可复现且局部持久的故障模式，这表明平均情况下的基准测试可能掩盖世界模型驱动控制中存在的重要脆弱性。

The Imitator Game: Benchmarking Robot Imitative Ability Beyond Action Prediction VLA

Imitator Game IG-10K intent-level imitation functional substitution human-video conditioned policy

简要说明：该论文提出了“模仿者游戏”，一个四层基准测试和数据集（IG-10K），用于评估机器人超越动作重放的模仿能力，揭示了即使有人类视频条件，当前模型在功能替代（意图级模仿）上仍然失败。

TLDR: The paper introduces The Imitator Game, a four-level benchmark and dataset (IG-10K) for evaluating robot imitation beyond action replay, revealing that current models fail at functional substitution (intent-level imitation) even with human-video conditioning.

Abstract: Humans imitate at the level of intent: given a demonstration, we infer its goal and carry it out with whatever tools, objects, and layouts are at hand. Current robot policies instead learn observation-to-action mappings from visual inputs and language instructions, without explicitly inferring the demonstrated task. Learning from human video thus remains largely trajectory-level: models can replay motions in near-identical scenes, but still struggle to imitate what the demonstrator intends rather than merely what they do. We introduce The Imitator Game, a four-level benchmark (L0-L3) that progressively widens the gap between the human demonstration and the robot's own scene, isolating where trajectory replay ceases to suffice and task understanding becomes necessary. We pair it with IG-10K, the largest environment-aligned paired human-robot dataset to date and the only one instantiated across all four levels in both real and simulated settings (20,000+ paired episodes, 50+ tasks, 6 domains), and Imitator Arena, an open platform for blind A/B human evaluation. Across nine state-of-the-art models, performance is stable from L0 to L2 but collapses at L3, identifying functional substitution - achieving the same intent through a different object affordance - as the decisive barrier to intent-level imitation. Human-video-conditioned models outperform caption-conditioned ones, yet every model falls below 13% zero-shot success on unseen tasks; fine-tuning IG-10K-pretrained models with only $10$ paired human-robot demonstrations yields large gains that grow with pretraining scale. The project website and access to Imitator Arena are available at https://imitator-game.github.io.

摘要（中文）：人类在意图层面进行模仿：面对一个示范，我们会推断其目标，并用当前可用的工具、物体和布局来执行它。当前的机器人策略则是从视觉输入和语言指令中学习观察到动作的映射，而不显式推断被示范的任务。因此，从人类视频中学习仍然主要停留在轨迹层面：模型可以在高度相似的场景中重放动作，但仍难以模仿示范者意图完成的任务，而不仅仅是其执行的动作。我们提出了“模仿者游戏”，这是一个包含四个级别（L0-L3）的基准测试，逐步扩大人类示范与机器人自身场景之间的差距，从而区分出轨迹重放不再足够、任务理解变得必要的临界点。我们为其配套构建了IG-10K数据集——迄今为止规模最大且与环境对齐的人机配对数据集，也是唯一一个在真实与模拟场景中均实例化所有四个级别的数据集（超过20,000个配对样本、50多项任务、6个领域），以及“模仿者竞技场”，一个用于盲法A/B人类评估的开放平台。在九个最先进模型上，从L0到L2级别性能保持稳定，但在L3级别急剧下降，这揭示出功能性替换——通过不同的物体功能实现相同意图——是意图级模仿的关键障碍。基于人类视频条件的模型优于基于字幕条件的模型，但所有模型在未见任务上的零样本成功率均低于13%；仅用10个人机配对示范微调IG-10K预训练模型，即可获得显著提升，且该提升随预训练规模增大而增大。项目网站及模仿者竞技场访问入口详见 https://imitator-game.github.io。

Meta-Ctrl: Guaranteed Plan Generation by Decoupling Syntactic and Semantic Constraints VLA

constrained decoding robot plan generation syntactic and semantic constraints meta-tokens embodied agent

简要说明：Meta-Ctrl 是一个约束解码框架，确保机器人规划生成满足语法和语义约束，使小型开源语言模型在任务规划基准上超越 GPT-4 并在真实机器人上成功执行。

TLDR: Meta-Ctrl is a constrained-decoding framework that guarantees syntactic and semantic constraints for robot plan generation, enabling small open-weight LMs to outperform GPT-4 on task planning benchmarks and succeed on a real robot.

Abstract: LLMs generate fluent plans for robots but routinely violate the syntactic and se8mantic constraints they must satisfy to execute, and existing remedies trade formal guarantees against plan quality: soft methods (affordance scoring, grounded decoding) give no guarantee, while symbolic planners (LLM+P) discard the LM's commonsense. We propose \textbf{Meta-Ctrl}, a constrained-decoding framework that guarantees the encoded constraints while preserving the base LM's plan quality. Meta-Ctrl introduces \emph{meta-tokens}---a compact vocabulary of grounded actions---enforcing syntax at the token level and semantics (preconditions, goals, ordering) at the action level, an exact factorization that cuts the memory of constrained decoding from over 107TB to under 2GB. With it, a small open-weight LM becomes competitive where it otherwise sits at the bottom of the leaderboard: on WAH-NL under the LoTa-Bench protocol it reaches the highest reported subgoal success rate, exceeding GPT-4's, with consistent gains across the Embodied Agent Interface. We further demonstrate it on a real tabletop robot, where every generated plan satisfies its preconditions and goals by construction. Project website: https://meta-ctrlg.github.io/.

Inferring Action from Future Latent State for Robotic Manipulation WorldModel

world-action model future latent state robotic manipulation action inference video generation free

简要说明：本文提出DELE-w0.5，一种通过紧凑的未来隐状态推断机器人动作的世界-动作模型，避免了密集视频生成，在长程操作任务上以更低成本和延迟取得优异性能。

TLDR: This paper proposes DELE-w0.5, a world-action model for robotic manipulation that infers actions from a compact future latent state instead of generating dense video frames, achieving superior performance on long-horizon tasks with lower training cost and latency.

Abstract: World-Action Models (WAMs) build robot control on video-generation backbones, which jointly predict dense future visual trajectories and robot actions. We argue that video generation is an unnecessary intermediate objective for world-action modeling. For robotic manipulation, the goal of a world model is not to reproduce how the world looks at every intermediate moment, but to predict the state that the world will reach after an action is executed. The intermediate frames only describe the visual transition between physical states, which consumes substantial model capacity and computation, but do not directly specify the physical outcome that the robot action is intended to produce. In this paper, we propose DELE-w0.5, which infers robot actions from predicted future states without relying on video generation. Concretely, DELE-w0.5 infers the action sequence from its corresponding compact future latent state. The future latent state captures the action-relevant physical outcome of robot interaction and serves as an explicit bridge between world modeling and action generation. The core design principle of DELE-w0.5 is to model how the physical world changes under robot actions, rather than how its visual appearance evolves frame by frame. This formulation removes the high-dimensional visual redundancy introduced by dense video representations, and it therefore enables cheaper training and low-latency inference. Across 480 real-robot trials on four long-horizon manipulation tasks, our DELE-w0.5 achieves the best performance among all compared policies, attaining 62.5 overall full-task success and 81.3 macro ordered-stage progress, outperforming the strongest baseline by 47.5 and 30.7 percentage points, respectively.

摘要（中文）：世界-行动模型（WAMs）基于视频生成主干网络构建机器人控制，可联合预测密集的未来视觉轨迹与机器人动作。我们认为，对于世界-行动建模而言，视频生成并非必要的中介目标。在机器人操作中，世界模型的目标并非再现世界在每一中间时刻的视觉呈现，而是预测动作执行后世界将达成的状态。中间帧仅描述物理状态间的视觉过渡，消耗大量模型容量与计算资源，却未能直接指明机器人动作旨在产生的物理结果。本文提出DELE-w0.5，该模型通过预测未来状态推断机器人动作，无需依赖视频生成。具体而言，DELE-w0.5从其对应的紧凑未来潜在状态中推断动作序列。未来潜在状态捕捉了与动作相关的机器人交互物理结果，并作为世界建模与动作生成之间的显式桥梁。DELE-w0.5的核心设计原则是建模物理世界在机器人动作作用下的变化方式，而非其视觉外观逐帧演变的模式。这一设计消除了密集视频表示引入的高维视觉冗余，从而降低了训练成本并实现低延迟推理。在四个长时域操作任务的480次真实机器人试验中，DELE-w0.5在所有对比策略中取得最佳性能，全任务成功率达62.5%，宏观有序阶段进展率达81.3%，分别领先最强基线模型47.5个百分点和30.7个百分点。

WAM-OPD: On-Policy Distillation for World Action Models WorldModel

on-policy distillation world action model video-first WAM flow-matching regularizer deployment-consistent

简要说明：提出WAM-OPD，一种在线策略蒸馏方法，通过冻结教师模型在学生部署一致的历史轨迹上监督动作生成，修复视频优先的世界动作模型，在RoboTwin任务上取得初步成功。

TLDR: Introduces WAM-OPD, an on-policy distillation method that repairs video-first world action models by using a frozen teacher to supervise student actions on deployment-consistent histories, showing preliminary success on RoboTwin tasks.

Abstract: World action models (WAMs) couple visual future prediction with robot action generation, but accelerated students can lose task capabilities during distillation and later encounter states that are poorly represented by offline data. We study whether on-policy distillation (OPD) can repair such a student without requiring sparse-reward reinforcement learning. We introduce WAM-OPD, a deployment-consistent post-training recipe for a video-first WAM. The student acts in the environment and therefore determines the history distribution. A frozen teacher labels those student histories with coherent video and action targets, while the student action branch is trained under its own generated video plan, as it is at deployment. Joint video and action losses update lightweight adapters in the shared backbone, together with an action flow-matching regularizer. In preliminary RoboTwin 2.0 studies on two tasks, the released one-video/one-action-step Flash-WAM improves from 0.0% to 58.3% success on HANDOVER MIC, and from 16.7% to 33.3% on PUT OBJECT CABINET. These task-specific results are an initial capability proof rather than evidence of broad or uniform generalization. They nevertheless suggest that dense teacher supervision on student-induced histories is a promising post-training interface for video-first WAMs.

摘要（中文）：世界动作模型（WAMs）将视觉未来预测与机器人动作生成相结合，但经过加速训练的学生模型可能在知识蒸馏过程中丧失任务能力，并随后遭遇离线数据难以有效表征的状态。我们研究基于策略的蒸馏（OPD）是否能在无需稀疏奖励强化学习的情况下修复此类学生模型。本文提出WAM-OPD，一种面向视频优先WAM的部署一致性后训练方案。学生模型在环境中执行动作，从而决定历史数据分布。冻结参数的教师模型为这些学生历史数据标注连贯的视频与动作目标，同时学生动作分支在自身生成的视频规划下进行训练（与部署阶段一致）。联合视频与动作损失函数更新共享骨干网络中的轻量适配器，并辅以动作流匹配正则化项。在RoboTwin 2.0两项任务的初步研究中，已发布的单视频/单动作步Flash-WAM在HANDOVER MIC任务上的成功率从0.0%提升至58.3%，在PUT OBJECT CABINET任务上从16.7%提升至33.3%。这些任务特定结果仅作为初始能力验证，而非广泛或统一泛化能力的证据。尽管如此，该结果表明，对学生模型生成历史数据施加密集教师监督，是视频优先WAM颇具前景的后训练接口方案。
