OpenNWM: A Generalist Navigation World Model with Latent Action Pretraining WorldModel

简要说明：OpenNWM 通过在大规模无动作导航视频上进行潜在动作预训练，构建通用导航世界模型，提升了跨多种环境的视觉预测和导航性能。

TLDR: OpenNWM introduces latent action pretraining on large-scale action-free navigation videos to build a generalist navigation world model, improving visual prediction and downstream navigation performance across diverse environments.

Abstract: Navigation world models predict future observations conditioned on navigation actions to support planning, but their generalization across real-world environments remains constrained by the limited coverage of action-labeled data. Action-free navigation videos provide diverse visual experience, yet lack the recorded actions required for action-conditioned training. We introduce OpenNWM, a generalist navigation world model with latent action pretraining. It learns universal latent actions from large-scale action-free videos through visual prediction pretraining, and further aligns them with physical motions via action-conditioned post-training. To support video pretraining, we curate NavAnywhere, a large-scale action-free navigation video dataset comprising 17.5 million visual observations from 15 heterogeneous sources. It captures navigation by ground robots, humans, and drones, with diverse viewpoints and motion patterns across five broad scene types: residential areas, public and commercial spaces, urban settings, parks, and natural landscapes. Experiments demonstrate improved visual prediction on in-domain and out-of-domain benchmarks, together with improved navigation performance. These results show that latent-action pretraining enables diverse, action-free videos to improve dynamics learning and generalization for open-world navigation.

ODE Policy: Robot Learning via Action Dynamics, Not Actions VLA

neural ODE action dynamics vision-language-action model world action model imitation learning

简要说明：ODE Policy 用神经ODE学习动作的动力学而非直接预测动作，从而保证执行轨迹的连续性。其在14项真实世界任务中作为视觉运动策略、VLA/WAM动作头及RL actor，实现了最高成功率且更高效。

TLDR: ODE Policy replaces direct action prediction with learning action dynamics via a neural ODE, producing continuous trajectories by construction. It is evaluated as a visuomotor policy, VLA/WAM action head, and RL actor across 14 real-world tasks, achieving state-of-the-art success with higher efficiency.

Abstract: Most robot policies, whether trained by imitation learning (IL) or reinforcement learning (RL), map observations to action values at discrete control steps and leave the continuity of physical motion to be learned implicitly. We propose ODE policy, which learns the dynamics of the action rather than the action itself. A neural ordinary differential equation (ODE) learns the vector field of the action by matching the integrated actions to the targets over short action chunks in IL and single control steps in RL. A lightweight diversity head captures multi-modal behavior by randomizing the direction of the field. At deployment, each action is integrated from the previous one, so the executed trajectory is continuous by construction. We evaluate it as a visuomotor policy, as the action head of vision-language-action (VLA) and world action models (WAMs), and as the actor of model-based RL against 14 baselines on unimanual, bimanual, dexterous, locomotion, and loco-manipulation tasks. In IL, against flow matching, it reaches 5.9$\times$ the success, 5.4$\times$ the generalization, and 3.9$\times$ the mode balance after one epoch, and generates actions 10.8$\times$ faster. It mostly moves more smoothly than human demonstrations. On 14 real-world tasks across single-arm, dual-arm, dexterous, and humanoid robots, it achieves the highest success rate. In RL, it further speeds up SuperMimic for humanoid motion tracking and smooths its actions. Efficient in learning, data, parameters, and execution, ODE policy offers a promising path to scaling robot learning.

摘要（中文）：大多数机器人策略，无论是通过模仿学习（IL）还是强化学习（RL）训练的，都在离散控制步骤上将观测映射到动作值，而将物理运动的连续性留给隐式学习。我们提出ODE策略，它学习动作的动力学而非动作本身。神经常微分方程（ODE）通过在IL中的短动作块和RL中的单控制步骤上将积分动作与目标匹配来学习动作的向量场。一个轻量级多样性头通过随机化场的方向来捕捉多模态行为。在部署时，每个动作从前一个动作积分而来，因此执行的轨迹在构造上就是连续的。我们将其作为视觉运动策略、视觉-语言-动作（VLA）和世界动作模型（WAM）的动作头，以及基于模型的RL的 actor，在单臂、双臂、灵巧、运动和移动操作任务上与14个基线进行评测。在IL中，相较于流匹配，它在一次epoch后达到5.9倍的成功率、5.4倍的泛化能力和3.9倍的模式平衡，且生成动作速度快10.8倍。它的运动大多比人类演示更平滑。在涵盖单臂、双臂、灵巧和类人机器人的14个真实世界任务上，它取得了最高成功率。在RL中，它进一步加速了SuperMimic用于类人运动跟踪并使其动作更平滑。在学习、数据、参数和执行方面均高效，ODE策略为规模化机器人学习提供了一条有前景的路径。

HuG: Learning Humanoid Whole-Body Affordance-Aware Grasping with Active Perception HumanoidEmbodied

简要说明：HuG 是一个面向人形机器人的全身可供性感知抓取框架，通过信念驱动的主动感知、基于点云的可供性策略以及控制器在环的运动动力学过滤，实现仅用仿真训练即可在多层货架任务上达到 87.5% 的真实世界成功率。

TLDR: HuG is a whole-body affordance-aware grasping framework for humanoids that couples belief-driven active perception with a point-cloud-conditioned affordance policy and controller-in-the-loop kinodynamic filtering, achieving 87.5% real-world success on multi-level shelf tasks trained purely in simulation.

Abstract: Acquiring objects in human environments is hard for humanoids: targets are partially occluded under egocentric viewpoints, and reaching them demands coordinated whole-body motion that keeps the target visible and reachable while the base stays stable. We present HuG, a framework for whole-body affordance-aware grasping with active perception that couples perception and action through a Perceive-Align-Grasp pipeline: belief-driven active perception resolves occlusion by treating viewpoint as an explicit decision variable; a point-cloud-conditioned affordance policy proposes whole-body grasps; and an affordance-aware alignment stage repositions the body to the most executable configuration. A key insight is that, on a floating base, kinematic validity is not enough: we curate training data with controller-in-the-loop dynamics simulation, rejecting 73% of kinematically valid grasps that would destabilize the deployment controller, so the policy learns kinodynamically feasible affordances. Trained entirely in simulation with zero real demonstrations, HuG attains 87.5% real-world success on a multi-level shelf task, generalizes to novel objects, and holds at least 73% success at every tested height. Beyond generalizing within its training distribution, the policy extrapolates spatially: at scale in simulation it succeeds at table heights well below any seen during training, indicating that kinodynamically filtered synthetic data grounds affordances in object geometry and whole-body feasibility rather than in memorized configurations.

Vision-Language Models Are Joint Denoisers in Robotic Action Generation VLA

简要说明：JADE 让预训练视觉语言模型与扩散动作专家联合去噪动作潜变量，VLM 在每一步读取含噪动作并通过门控交叉注意力提供随生成过程演化的引导，在 RoboDojo、RoboTwin 2.0 和真实双臂任务上提升了 VLA 策略性能。

TLDR: JADE enables a pretrained VLM and a diffusion action expert to jointly denoise action latents, with the VLM reading noisy actions and providing evolving guidance via gated cross-attention, improving VLA policy performance on RoboDojo, RoboTwin 2.0, and real-world bimanual tasks.

Abstract: Diffusion-based vision-language-action (VLA) models cascade pretrained vision-language models (VLMs) with diffusion action experts, but typically restrict the VLM to providing static conditioning. This separation limits how much pretrained perception and reasoning can guide action generation, especially in out-of-distribution settings. We introduce JADE, a Joint Action DEnoiser that enables the VLM and action expert to jointly refine actions. Our key insight is that structured action latents allow the VLM to read the current noisy action together with visual observations and language instructions. At every denoising step, the VLM contextualizes the noisy action and guides the action expert through gated cross-attention, so that its guidance evolves with the action being generated. We further supervise the VLM to recover clean action latents from noisy ones, which gives it a direct action-learning signal and couples perception with control. Additionally, joint denoising enables JADE to generate actions with fewer sampling steps, helping offset the additional cost of VLM participation. An adaptive step-size schedule further balances task performance and inference latency. On RoboDojo, JADE achieves an average success rate of 12.67%, the highest among the evaluated $π_{0.5}$-based methods, and is competitive with recent VLA and WAM foundation models pretrained at scale. It also improves performance on RoboTwin 2.0 and raises the average success rate across four real-world bimanual tasks by 20.8 percentage points compared with $π_{0.5}$.

Xiaomi Auto World Model 2.0: Causal World Models for Real-World Driving and Embodied Intelligence WorldModel

简要说明：本文介绍了小米自动驾驶世界模型2.0，一种统一的流式因果世界模型，用于物理AI，可生成视图一致的未来状态，支持闭环仿真，并推动世界动作模型在驾驶和具身智能中的应用。

TLDR: This paper introduces Xiaomi Auto World Model 2.0, a unified streaming causal world model for physical AI that generates view-consistent future states, supports closed-loop simulation, and enables World Action Model applications for driving and embodied intelligence.

Abstract: We present Xiaomi Auto World Model 2.0, a unified streaming causal world model for Physical AI. It models and generates causal physical evolution in the real world, and unifies heterogeneous conditioning inputs for view-consistent generation. The architecture integrates View-Temporal Attention (VTA) to capture cross-view spatial dependencies and temporal dynamics, together with Multi-view Rotary Position Encoding (RoPE) to encode positional relationships across views and time. WorldRec 2.0 reconstructs static and dynamic 3D Gaussians from multi-view, multi-temporal observations in a single forward pass and uses their renderings as explicit scene context to preserve geometry and appearance during long-horizon generation. Conditioning inputs are fused through VAE latent concatenation in a shared representation space. We incorporate Gated Linear Attention (GLA) into the temporal layers of VTA for efficient streaming inference, reducing computation and KV-cache memory during multi-view rollouts. A physical-interaction data pipeline covers diverse interactions and long-tailed events. From Pre-train onward, these data are combined with large-scale videos and heterogeneous visual modalities to learn visual, geometric, and dynamic priors. During Mid-train, training across diverse view configurations further improves the model's ability to capture how actions and object states shape subsequent scene evolution. After distillation-based Post-train, we apply the model to closed-loop simulation, closed-loop training, and World Action Model (WAM) applications. These applications generate physically consistent future states and support world-state prediction, policy learning, and action generation for Physical AI.

简要说明：该论文识别了扩散驾驶VLA中的深度-解码分离现象，并提出了场景自适应的读出MoE和自适应解码策略，在提升规划质量的同时实现最高6倍的端到端加速。

TLDR: The paper identifies a depth-decoding dissociation in diffusion driving VLAs and proposes a scene-adaptive readout MoE and adaptive decoding policy to improve planning quality while achieving up to 6x end-to-end speedup.

Abstract: Diffusion driving vision-language-action models repeatedly execute the backbone at every denoising stage, incurring substantial inference cost, while it remains unclear whether all scenes require the same decoding computation. To distinguish when a trajectory becomes readable from how much further refinement it requires, we analyze trajectory readout across network depth and decoding progress. We identify a depth-decoding dissociation in which late-layer representations already support useful trajectory readout after the first forward sweep, whereas early-layer representations benefit more from continued decoding. We further find that both the preferred readout layer and decoding depth vary across scenes. Based on these observations, we propose a scene-adaptive readout and decoding framework. A scene-conditioned readout MoE adaptively weights and combines trajectory predictions from layer-specific late-layer representations after a single backbone sweep, while an adaptive decoding policy applies additional native refinement only when required by the scene. We evaluate our framework on two diffusion driving VLAs across the Waymo E2E and nuScenes benchmarks. Our method improves validation RFS by up to 0.56 while also improving test performance, and achieves an end-to-end speedup of up to $6.0\times$ on Waymo E2E, while consistently reducing trajectory error on nuScenes. These results demonstrate that scene-level readout and decoding allocation improves planning quality while avoiding unnecessary decoding. Our code is available at https://github.com/sjyu001/SARD

Back to the Future: Predicting Long-Horizon Dynamics with Retrospective Correction for Disturbance-Aware Retrieval Manipulation

disturbance-aware retrieval retrospective correction relational dynamics long-horizon prediction dense clutter

简要说明：DARE提出了一种结合几何感知关系动力学与回溯校正的框架，用于密集杂乱环境下的长期扰动预测，并指导滚动时域规划器进行物体取回。

TLDR: DARE introduces a geometry-aware relational dynamics framework with retrospective correction for long-horizon disturbance prediction, guiding a receding-horizon planner for object retrieval in dense clutter.

Abstract: Retrieving objects from densely packed storage requires anticipating how contact disturbs surrounding objects when limited clearance prevents lifting the target over clutter. However, contact can propagate beyond the target's immediate neighbors, while accumulated prediction errors make learned dynamics unreliable over long planning horizons. We introduce DARE (Disturbance-Aware REtrieval), a geometry-aware relational dynamics framework that uses retrospective correction to improve long-horizon prediction for disturbance-aware retrieval. DARE encodes compact object descriptors and predicts action-conditioned disturbance propagation through object-level message passing. When a sparse observation becomes available, a learned corrector uses the prediction discrepancy to amend an earlier predicted state and reruns the intervening dynamics, propagating the correction through the predicted contact chain. These predictions guide a receding-horizon planner that balances retrieval progress against displacement and rotation of surrounding objects, permitting necessary contact while discouraging harmful disturbance.
