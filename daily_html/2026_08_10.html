SG-WAM: Text-Grounded and Spatial-aware Semantic Guidance for World-Action Models WorldModel

world-action models vision-language model semantic guidance robotic manipulation instruction grounding

简要说明：SG-WAM 提出了一种基于 VLM 的语义规划器，将文本锚定和空间感知的语义预知注入世界动作模型，从而提升指令遵循能力和操作精度。

TLDR: SG-WAM introduces a VLM-based semantic planner that injects text-grounded and spatial-aware semantic foresight into world-action models, improving instruction-following and manipulation precision.

Abstract: World-Action Models (WAMs) have emerged as a promising paradigm for robotic manipulation. However, most existing WAMs generate future videos and actions by relying mainly on visual cues rather than language instructions, since off-the-shelf text encoders embed instructions independently of visual observations. As a result, the videos predicted by these WAMs are often semantically misaligned with their corresponding language instructions, which degrades the accuracy of the predicted actions. To overcome this limitation, we propose SG-WAM, a semantic guidance method for world-action models that leverages a vision-language model (VLM) as a semantic planner to enhance the instruction-grounding capacity of world-action models. Specifically, we train a VLM-based planner to predict text-grounded and spatial-aware semantic foresight. The text-grounded semantic foresight grounds the instruction by identifying the correct target objects, and the spatial-aware semantic foresight provides the scene geometry for precise manipulation. We then inject this foresight into the world-action model as high-level semantic guidance, ensuring that both future-video generation and action prediction faithfully follow the language instruction. Extensive experiments in simulation and the real world demonstrate the superiority of our semantic guidance method, showcasing precise manipulation and strong instruction-following capabilities.

摘要（中文）：世界动作模型（WAMs）已成为机器人操作领域一种前景广阔的新范式。然而，现有的大多数WAMs主要依赖视觉线索而非语言指令来生成未来视频和动作，因为现成的文本编码器在嵌入指令时独立于视觉观测。这导致这些WAMs预测的视频往往在语义上与对应的语言指令不一致，从而降低了预测动作的准确性。为克服这一局限，我们提出SG-WAM，一种面向世界动作模型的语义引导方法，通过利用视觉语言模型（VLM）作为语义规划器来增强世界动作模型的指令扎根能力。具体而言，我们训练一个基于VLM的规划器来预测具有文本扎根和空间感知能力的语义预见。文本扎根的语义预见通过识别正确的目标物体来锚定指令，而空间感知的语义预见则提供用于精确操作的场景几何信息。随后，我们将这种预见作为高层语义引导注入世界动作模型，确保未来视频生成和动作预测均严格遵循语言指令。在仿真和真实世界中的大量实验表明，我们的语义引导方法具有优越性，展现出精确的操作能力和强大的指令遵循能力。

Population-Scalable Multi-Agent World Modeling WorldModel

multi-agent world model population-agnostic rendering cross-view consistency scalable simulation interactive generation

简要说明：本文提出Khora，一种多智能体世界模型，通过解耦世界状态演化与视觉渲染，实现推理时无需重新训练即可扩展至任意数量智能体。

TLDR: This paper introduces Khora, a multi-agent world model that decouples world-state evolution from visual rendering to enable inference-time scaling to an arbitrary number of agents without retraining.

Abstract: World models have recently achieved impressive progress in visual prediction and interactive generation, but extending them to multi-agent environments introduces a fundamental scalability challenge. Existing methods generally assume a fixed number of agents during training and inference, which ties the model to a pre-determined agent population and limits inference-time scalability. Our key insight is that cross-view consistency should arise from a shared world state whose evolution does not assume a predefined number of agents, while agent-specific observations should be generated by querying this state through a unified rendering interface. Based on this insight, we propose Khora, a scalable multi-agent world model that supports inference-time expansion to arbitrary numbers of agents without retraining. Our framework decouples world-state evolution from visual rendering and introduces a population-agnostic rendering mechanism for incorporating other agent information. This design maintains cross-view consistency through the shared world state rather than through dense interactions among observation streams inside the expensive video generator, enabling approximately linear practical scaling with the number of queried views. Qualitative experiments demonstrate that our approach generalizes to unseen numbers of agents while maintaining visual quality and multi-agent consistency. We further implement a real-time interactive system to demonstrate scalable open-world simulation.

摘要（中文）：世界模型在视觉预测与交互式生成领域近期取得了显著进展，但将其拓展至多智能体环境却引入了一个根本性的可扩展性挑战。现有方法通常假设训练与推理阶段的智能体数量固定，这使得模型与预设的智能体规模绑定，限制了推理时的可扩展性。我们的核心洞察在于：跨视角一致性应源自共享世界状态的演变，而该状态的演化无需预设智能体数量；同时，特定于智能体的观测结果应通过统一的渲染接口查询该状态来生成。基于这一洞察，我们提出了Khora——一种可扩展的多智能体世界模型，支持在无需重新训练的情况下，将推理时的智能体数量扩展至任意规模。我们的框架将世界状态演化与视觉渲染解耦，并引入了一种与群体无关的渲染机制以整合其他智能体信息。该设计通过共享世界状态（而非在昂贵的视频生成器内部依赖观测流之间的密集交互）来维持跨视角一致性，从而实现与查询视角数量近似线性的实际扩展。定性实验表明，我们的方法在保持视觉质量与多智能体一致性的前提下，能够泛化至未见过的智能体数量。我们进一步实现了实时交互系统，以展示可扩展的开放世界仿真能力。

Goal-oriented Navigation Instruction Generation with Tour Video Priors VLN

Navigation Instruction Generation Curriculum Learning Ego-centric Video Vision-and-Language Navigation Multimodal Large Language Models

简要说明：该论文提出了VideoNIG任务和基准，从第一视角旅游视频生成导航指令，并设计了双阶段课程学习框架，提升了指令质量及下游VLN代理的可执行性。

TLDR: This paper introduces VideoNIG, a task and benchmark for generating navigation instructions from ego-centric tour videos, and proposes a two-stage Curriculum Learning framework that improves instruction quality and executability for downstream VLN agents.

Abstract: Navigation Instruction Generation (NIG) aims to produce step-by-step natural language instructions for navigation guidance. Existing studies primarily treat NIG as an auxiliary task for vision-andlanguage navigation (VLN), focusing on data augmentation or multi-task learning. However, generating navigation instructions from compact environmental priors requires meticulous spatial reasoning, especially when the target route does not simply follow the demonstrated tour, and remains challenging for current multimodal models. In this work, we introduce VideoNIG, a goal-oriented video-grounded NIG task that generates navigation instructions from ego-centric tour videos, an initial observation, and a textual or visual goal, without relying on intermediate representations such as graphs and maps. We instantiate VideoNIG in a controlled simulator benchmark with 60K tour videos across continuous indoor environments and 37K multimodal prompts with progressive difficulty levels. We further introduce a diagnostic evaluation protocol that combines text similarity, choice-based spatial consistency tests, and downstream navigation execution. To address this task, we propose a two-stage Curriculum Learning framework that decomposes the learning into foundational motion perception and long-horizon navigation reasoning. Specifically, we first employ Action Warmup for spatial action-view alignment, followed by Complexity Progression using trajectories with increasing exploratory difficulty. Extensive experiments show that existing MLLMs struggle with VideoNIG, while our approach significantly improves instruction quality across complementary diagnostic metrics. Finally, integrating VideoNIG-generated instructions with a VLN agent demonstrates the executability of this task formulation for end-to-end navigation.

摘要（中文）：导航指令生成（NIG）旨在为导航引导生成逐步的自然语言指令。现有研究主要将NIG视为视觉-语言导航（VLN）的辅助任务，重点关注数据增强或多任务学习。然而，从紧凑的环境先验信息中生成导航指令需要细致的空间推理，特别是当目标路径并非简单遵循演示巡游路径时，这对当前多模态模型仍构成挑战。本研究提出VideoNIG——一种面向目标的视频基础NIG任务，该任务从以自我为中心的巡游视频、初始观测以及文本或视觉目标中生成导航指令，无需依赖图或地图等中间表示。我们在受控仿真器基准中实例化VideoNIG，该基准包含连续室内环境中的6万个巡游视频，以及3.7万个难度递进的多模态提示。我们进一步引入诊断性评估协议，结合文本相似度、基于选择的空间一致性测试以及下游导航执行。为解决该任务，我们提出两阶段课程学习框架，将学习过程分解为基础运动感知与长期导航推理两个阶段。具体而言，我们首先采用动作预热训练进行空间动作-视角对齐，随后通过复杂度递进策略使用探索难度递增的轨迹进行训练。大量实验表明，现有多模态大语言模型在VideoNIG任务上表现欠佳，而我们的方法在互补性诊断指标上显著提升了指令质量。最后，将VideoNIG生成的指令与VLN智能体集成，验证了该任务范式在端到端导航中的可执行性。

360CityArena: A Realistic Virtual Urban Navigation Benchmark for Embodied Agents VLN

简要说明：360CityArena是一个基于东京秋叶原360度视频构建的逼真城市导航基准，包含175个任务，涵盖环境理解、路径推理和空间推理。

TLDR: 360CityArena is a photorealistic urban navigation benchmark for embodied agents, built from 360-degree videos of Tokyo's Akihabara district, with 175 tasks spanning environment understanding, path reasoning, and spatial reasoning.

Abstract: We present 360CityArena, a benchmark for evaluating the urban exploration capabilities of embodied agents within a photorealistic environment constructed from 360-degree videos. Existing outdoor benchmarks either lack sufficient photorealism or complexity, resulting in a considerable gap from real-world urban environments. 360CityArena is built on a realistic reconstruction of the Akihabara district in Tokyo, Japan, using 602 360-degree video segments covering 85 streets, and consists of 175 meticulously human-crafted tasks. It encompasses three task categories: Environment Understanding, Path Reasoning, and Spatial Reasoning, covering fundamental abilities required for urban exploration, such as localization, landmark search, path planning, and relational spatial reasoning, thereby enabling comprehensive evaluation in realistic urban scenes. Our evaluation using state-of-the-art LMM-based agents shows that even the strongest model, Gemini 2.5 Flash, performs far below human level (human: 77.3% vs. Gemini 2.5 Flash: 17.1%), revealing substantial challenges that remain in city-scale embodied navigation and reasoning. 360CityArena provides a necessary and challenging testbed for photorealistic urban-district navigation and spatial reasoning.

OccAnyScene: Towards Unified Indoor-Outdoor 3D Occupancy Predictio Perception3D

3D occupancy prediction cross-scene scene understanding depth foundation model frustum feature aggregation

简要说明：本文提出了跨场景3D语义占据预测这一新任务，要求单一模型处理异构的室内外场景，并提出了OccAnyScene，一个基于像素-视锥体中心的高斯框架，用于实现度量一致且场景自适应的图像到3D提升。

TLDR: This paper introduces Cross-Scene 3D Semantic Occupancy Prediction, a new task requiring a single model to handle heterogeneous indoor and outdoor scenes, and proposes OccAnyScene, a pixel-frustum-centered Gaussian framework for metric-consistent yet scene-adaptive image-to-3D lifting.

Abstract: 3D occupancy prediction is fundamental to scene understanding, yet existing 3D semantic occupancy methods are typically specialized to fixed scene types and occupancy protocols. We introduce Cross-Scene 3D Semantic Occupancy Prediction, a new task setting which requires a single model to handle heterogeneous indoor and outdoor scenes with varying cameras, spatial ranges, voxel specifications, and semantic taxonomies. This setting poses a fundamental challenge: achieving metric-consistent yet scene-adaptive image-to-3D lifting across varying camera configurations and scene scales. To address this challenge, we propose OccAnyScene, a pixel-frustum-centered Gaussian framework built upon a pretrained depth foundation model. Specifically, the framework employs Pixel-Aligned Frustum Feature Aggregation to construct a camera-aware frustum query for each feature pixel, and Frustum-Parameterized Gaussian Construction to decode each query into multiple Gaussians whose positions and sizes are constrained by the predicted pixel depth and corresponding frustum geometry. OccAnyScene sets new state-of-the-art results, achieving 59.92% mIoU on the indoor Occ-ScanNet and 23.06% mIoU on the outdoor SurroundOcc-nuScenes.

摘要（中文）：三维占据预测是场景理解的基础，但现有的三维语义占据方法通常针对固定的场景类型和占据协议。我们提出跨场景三维语义占据预测这一新任务设定，要求单一模型能够处理室内外异构场景，这些场景在相机配置、空间范围、体素规格和语义分类体系上均存在差异。该设定带来一个基本挑战：如何在变化的相机配置和场景尺度下，实现度量一致且场景自适应的图像到三维提升。为应对该挑战，我们提出OccAnyScene，一种以像素截锥体为中心的高斯框架，构建于预训练深度基础模型之上。具体而言，该框架采用像素对齐截锥体特征聚合，为每个特征像素构建相机感知的截锥体查询，并通过截锥体参数化高斯构造将每个查询解码为多个高斯体，其位置和尺寸受预测像素深度及对应截锥体几何约束。OccAnyScene在室内Occ-ScanNet上达到59.92% mIoU，在室外SurroundOcc-nuScenes上达到23.06% mIoU，创下新的最优结果。

RayLift: Lifting Complementary Ray-Wise Evidence with 3D Geometry Priors for Semantic Scene Completion AutonomousDriving

semantic scene completion stereo depth 3D geometry priors ray evidence autonomous driving

简要说明：RayLift 提出了一种框架，将立体几何作为度量参考，并通过互补的射线证据自适应地恢复三维结构，用于基于相机的三维语义场景补全，面向自动驾驶和机器人。

TLDR: RayLift introduces a framework that leverages stereo geometry as a metric reference and adaptively recovers 3D structures via complementary ray evidence for camera-based 3D semantic scene completion, targeting autonomous driving and robotics.

Abstract: Camera-based 3D semantic scene completion (SSC) provides comprehensive scene understanding for autonomous driving and robotics. However, existing methods often treat stereo depth estimates as deterministic geometric constraints, causing depth uncertainty and local correspondence errors to propagate directly into voxel representations. To address this issue, we propose RayLift, a framework that uses stereo geometry as a metric reference while incorporating complementary ray evidence to recover reliable 3D structures adaptively. RayLift first employs a Complementary Context Encoder that extracts geometry-aware priors from a frozen 3D vision foundation model, thereby enriching the scene context. It then introduces a Depth Ray Evidence Lifter module that jointly models geometric dissimilarity, depth confidence, and spatial uncertainty to adaptively sample and weight candidate surface locations along each camera ray. Finally, a Semantic-Aware Voxel Integrator injects the resulting ray evidence into voxel features by explicitly modeling their spatial support. Extensive experiments on SemanticKITTI and SSCBench-KITTI-360 demonstrate that RayLift achieves competitive performance and consistently outperforms existing methods.

摘要（中文）：基于相机的3D语义场景补全（SSC）可为自动驾驶和机器人技术提供全面的场景理解能力。然而现有方法往往将立体深度估计视为确定性几何约束，导致深度不确定性和局部对应误差直接传播至体素表征。针对该问题，我们提出RayLift框架，该框架以立体几何作为度量基准，同时融合互补光线证据以自适应恢复可靠3D结构。RayLift首先采用互补上下文编码器，从冻结的3D视觉基础模型中提取几何感知先验，从而丰富场景上下文信息；随后引入深度光线证据提升模块，通过联合建模几何差异度、深度置信度与空间不确定性，沿每条相机光线自适应采样并加权候选表面位置；最后，语义感知体素集成器通过显式建模光线证据的空间支撑关系，将其注入体素特征。在SemanticKITTI与SSCBench-KITTI-360上的大量实验表明，RayLift取得了具有竞争力的性能，并持续优于现有方法。

Action- and Language-Conditioned Video Assessment for Embodied Control VLA

trajectory evaluation action-conditioned video assessment language-conditioned feedback vision-language model embodied control

简要说明：ALVA 使用预训练的 VLM 分两阶段评估基于动作和语言的视频轨迹，为模拟具身控制提供保守且可解释的反馈。

TLDR: ALVA uses a pre-trained VLM in two stages to assess video trajectories conditioned on actions and language, providing conservative and interpretable feedback for simulated embodied control.

Abstract: Vision-based embodied agents executing multi-step natural language instructions require feedback mechanisms that assess task progress over complete trajectories. Conventional approaches based on final-frame matching or continuous embedding similarity may overlook intermediate transitions that are necessary for determining whether an instruction has been completed. We propose ALVA (Action- and Language-Conditioned Video Assessment), a trajectory evaluator that conditions its assessment on visual observations, the executed action sequence, and the natural language instruction. The method uses a pre-trained vision-language model (VLM) in two stages: it first summarizes frame-to-frame visual transitions conditioned on the executed actions and then assesses the generated summary with respect to the instruction to produce a discrete trajectory-level progress score. In simulated 3D household environments, ALVA exhibits a conservative assessment pattern with near-zero false-positive rates. When used as terminal feedback for closed-loop policy optimization, it provides more effective feedback than the evaluated static image and embedding-based visual baselines and reduces the performance gap to a ground-truth oracle. These results support action- and language-conditioned video assessment as an interpretable feedback mechanism for the evaluated simulated embodied-control tasks.

摘要（中文）：基于视觉的具身智能体在执行多步自然语言指令时，需要能够评估完整轨迹任务进度的反馈机制。传统的基于最终帧匹配或连续嵌入相似度的方法可能会忽略判断指令是否完成所必需的中间状态转换。我们提出了ALVA（动作与语言条件视频评估），这是一种轨迹评估器，其评估依据包括视觉观测、已执行的动作序列以及自然语言指令。该方法采用预训练的视觉-语言模型（VLM），分两个阶段进行：首先，基于已执行的动作对帧间视觉转换进行总结；然后，根据指令对生成的总结进行评估，从而产生离散的轨迹级进度分数。在模拟的三维家居环境中，ALVA表现出保守的评估模式，假阳性率接近于零。当将其用作闭环策略优化的终端反馈时，它比所评估的静态图像和基于嵌入的视觉基线方法提供了更有效的反馈，并缩小了与真实标准结果之间的性能差距。这些结果支持将动作与语言条件视频评估作为所评估的模拟具身控制任务中一种可解释的反馈机制。
