ResWAM: Learning What Changes, Not What Stays, for World-Action Models VLA

visual residual learning world-action model vision-language-action policy conditional flow matching robot manipulation

简要说明：ResWAM 提出视觉残差学习方法，仅预测未来帧的特征变化而非完整帧，从而提高世界动作模型的策略学习效率和性能，在仿真和真实机器人任务中表现优异。

TLDR: ResWAM introduces visual residual learning for world-action models, predicting only feature changes rather than full future frames, which improves policy learning efficiency and performance on simulation and real-robot tasks.

Abstract: World-action models (WAMs) augment vision-language-action policies (VLAs) with future prediction so that the policy can anticipate how its actions will affect the physical world. Existing WAMs supervise complete future observations. However, observations typically change little over an action chunk, so the future loss is dominated by copying static background content rather than learning task-relevant robot and object motion, limiting policy learning. Moreover, existing WAMs rely on large video generation models or separate world models, incurring heavy training and inference compute that hinders closed-loop deployment. To improve policy performance, we propose visual residual learning, supervising only the feature change from the current frame to the future frame in a frozen DINOv3 space. Compared with complete future features, this residual learning target yields a higher feature rank with richer and more diverse representations of scene changes, and spatially concentrates on the robot arm and manipulated objects rather than the background. To further improve computational efficiency, we present ResWAM, a residual world-action model with 2.3B parameters in total, including a lightweight 0.5B dual-branch DiT that jointly trains an action branch and a residual branch via conditional flow matching. Without robot pretraining (i.e., pretraining on external robot action trajectories beyond the benchmark demonstrations), ResWAM achieves top average success rates on LIBERO-Plus (81.1%), RoboTwin 2.0 (93.8%), and RoboDojo (9.40% success, 13.41 score) at the lowest training (2.7-4.0 TFLOPs) and inference compute (1.0-1.4 TFLOPs), and leads real-robot Open, Put, and Stack (80.0% average, +10.0% over $π_{0.5}$) at 92.7 ms latency.

摘要（中文）：世界动作模型（WAMs）通过未来预测增强视觉-语言-动作策略（VLAs），使策略能够预判其动作对物理世界的影响。现有WAMs对完整的未来观测进行监督，然而，在动作片段内观测通常变化甚微，因此未来损失主要被静态背景内容的复制所主导，而非学习与任务相关的机器人和物体运动，从而限制了策略学习。此外，现有WAMs依赖大规模视频生成模型或独立的 world models，导致训练和推理计算开销沉重，阻碍了闭环部署。为提升策略性能，我们提出视觉残差学习，在冻结的 DINOv3 空间中仅监督当前帧到未来帧的特征变化。与完整的未来特征相比，该残差学习目标产生更高的特征秩，蕴含更丰富、更多样化的场景变化表示，并在空间上集中于机械臂和被操作物体，而非背景。为进一步提升计算效率，我们提出 ResWAM，一个总参数量为 2.3B 的残差世界动作模型，其中包含一个轻量级 0.5B 双分支 DiT，通过条件流匹配联合训练动作分支和残差分支。在不进行机器人预训练（即不利用基准演示之外的机器人动作轨迹进行预训练）的情况下，ResWAM 在 LIBERO-Plus（81.1%）、RoboTwin 2.0（93.8%）和 RoboDojo（成功率 9.40%，得分 13.41）上以最低的训练（2.7-4.0 TFLOPs）和推理计算量（1.0-1.4 TFLOPs）取得最高平均成功率，并在真实机器人 Open、Put 和 Stack 任务中以 92.7 ms 延迟取得领先表现（平均 80.0%，较 π₀.₅ 提升 10.0%）。

PanDWM: Panoramic 4D Driving World Models WorldModel

panoramic driving world model causal student knowledge distillation 4D Gaussians 360-degree consistency

简要说明：PanDWM将多相机驾驶视频拼接成360度全景带，并训练从教师模型蒸馏出的因果学生世界模型，以更高效地生成一致的4D驾驶场景。在nuScenes上相比多相机基线降低了FVD，同时速度提升8-46倍。

TLDR: PanDWM stitches multi-camera driving video into a single 360-degree panoramic band, and trains a causal student world model distilled from a teacher to generate consistent 4D driving scenes more efficiently. It improves FVD over multi-camera baselines on nuScenes while being 8-46x faster.

Abstract: Driving world models usually generate videos from six pinhole cameras with separate optical centres and small overlaps, and must learn to keep these views consistent. We present PanDWM, which stitches them into one $360^\circ$ panoramic band. Camera boundaries then fall inside one image, so keeping the views consistent means keeping the image continuous, and each pixel still records its camera ray. Conditioned on layouts, a causal student generates the band window by window in four steps; it is distilled from a causal teacher and trained on its own rollouts. A feed-forward decoder lifts each frame along these rays into 3D Gaussians, and static Gaussians from neighbouring frames complete them into 4D scenes. On nuScenes, scored in one stitched $360^\circ$ view, PanDWM has lower FID and FVD than the multi-camera baselines at $8$-$46\times$ their speed, and keeps its 50-step teacher's FID while lowering FVD by 22-25%.

摘要（中文）：驾驶世界模型通常从六个具有独立光学中心和较小重叠区域的针孔摄像头生成视频，并且必须学习保持这些视图的一致性。我们提出了PanDWM，它将这六个视图拼接成一个$360^\circ$全景带。摄像头边界随后落在一张图像内部，因此保持视图一致就意味着保持图像连续，同时每个像素仍然记录其对应的摄像头光线。在布局条件的约束下，一个因果学生模型以四步窗口方式逐窗口生成该全景带；它从因果教师模型中蒸馏得到，并在自身的展开轨迹上进行训练。一个前馈解码器将每一帧沿这些光线提升为3D高斯体，相邻帧的静态高斯体将它们补全为4D场景。在nuScenes数据集上，以拼接后的单一$360^\circ$视图进行评分，PanDWM的FID和FVD均低于多摄像头基线方法，同时速度是其$8$至$46$倍，并且在保持其50步教师模型FID的同时，将FVD降低了22-25%。

Cleanly-DINO: Efficiently Learning Visual Task Spaces for Robotics Manipulation

visual task spaces few demonstrations cleaning manipulation reward signal real-world robotic wiping

简要说明：Cleanly-DINO 从少量演示中学习清洁任务的视觉任务空间，通过将冻结的 ViT 特征投影到任务相关区域来拟合进度度量，并作为奖励信号用于强化学习及真实机器人擦拭任务。

TLDR: Cleanly-DINO learns visual task spaces from few demonstrations for cleaning tasks, fitting a projection onto frozen ViT features to isolate task-relevant progress, and is evaluated as a reward signal for RL and deployed in a real-world robotic wiping pipeline.

Abstract: Learning manipulation tasks from few demonstrations requires abstractions that capture the changes relevant to task completion, such as object poses in rigid-body manipulation. However, tasks such as cleaning are defined by changes in visual state rather than object pose, making the choice of task representation less clear. In this work, we propose Cleanly-DINO to learn visual task spaces from few demonstrations. Given a mask of a task-relevant region and frozen ViT features, Cleanly-DINO fits a projection to isolate a task space and fit a progression metric to it. We train the model on diverse cleaning examples and evaluate its ability to localize incomplete task regions and transfer across visual domains. We also study its utility for robot learning by using it as an observation encoder and reward signal in a reinforcement learning setting. Finally, we deploy our fitted model within a real-world robotic wiping pipeline to evaluate its use in guiding task execution.

摘要（中文）：从少量示范中学习操控任务，需要抽象出与任务完成相关的状态变化，例如刚体操控中的物体位姿。然而，诸如清洁等任务是由视觉状态的变化而非物体位姿来定义的，这使得任务表示的选择变得不那么明确。在本工作中，我们提出Cleanly-DINO，用于从少量示范中学习视觉任务空间。给定任务相关区域的掩码和冻结的ViT特征，Cleanly-DINO拟合一个投影以隔离任务空间，并对其拟合一个进度度量。我们在多样化的清洁示例上训练该模型，并评估其在定位未完成任务区域及跨视觉域迁移方面的能力。我们还通过将其用作强化学习设置中的观测编码器和奖励信号，研究其在机器人学习中的实用性。最后，我们在真实世界的机器人擦拭流程中部署所拟合的模型，以评估其在指导任务执行中的应用。

SAFER: Navigating the Exploration-Exploitation Dilemma in World-Model-Based Planning for Autonomous Driving AutonomousDriving

world model exploration-exploitation risk-aware planning offline learning closed-loop evaluation

简要说明：SAFER 是一个用于自动驾驶中基于世界模型规划的新框架，通过离线从 staged failures 中学习并用专家行为和价值注释状态，再在线细化模型，来解决探索-利用困境。它使用分块潜在世界模型和风险感知的基于采样的规划器，在 CARLA 和 MetaDrive 基准测试中取得了最先进的路线完成率和奖励。

TLDR: SAFER is a framework for world-model-based planning in autonomous driving that addresses the exploration-exploitation dilemma by learning offline from staged failures and annotating states with expert actions and values, then refining the model online. It uses a chunked latent world model and risk-aware sampling-based planner, achieving state-of-the-art route completion and reward in CARLA and MetaDrive benchmarks.

Abstract: An autonomous vehicle must foresee the consequences of its actions, especially in the rare, time-critical situations where crashes happen. A world model offers this foresight, but only within the coverage of its training data, which poses an exploration-exploitation dilemma. Within this coverage a planner can hardly improve on the data, and beyond it the model's errors mislead it. Logs of competent driving rarely contain the failures that would widen the coverage, and agents that learn from interaction gain them only by failing themselves, without being shown what to do instead. We propose SAFER, a framework that learns offline from failures that others make, judged by an expert, and keeps learning online from its own driving. Offline, partially trained agents and scripted, recoverable perturbations Stage failures in simulation, and an expert Annotates every visited state with its action and value, so that much of what the planner explores is covered before it drives. In planning, a chunked latent world model Foresees the consequences fast enough for real-time use, and a risk-aware sampling-based planner Evaluates candidate maneuvers by their return minus a bounded collision penalty. Online, a loop continuously Refines the model wherever this driving leaves the coverage of the offline data. In closed-loop evaluation against the compared methods, SAFER reaches the highest route completion and reward per step on the CARLA Leaderboard routes, 0.95 and 0.77 from a privileged bird's-eye view, and the highest episode reward in MetaDrive, 53.4 in regular and 49.0 in safety-critical traffic. Project page: https://ys-qu.github.io/safer-website.

摘要（中文）：自主驾驶车辆必须预见其行为带来的后果，尤其是在发生碰撞的罕见、时间紧迫的关键情境中。世界模型提供了这种预见能力，但其适用范围仅限于训练数据所覆盖的范围，这带来了探索与利用之间的两难困境。在该覆盖范围内，规划器难以在数据基础上有所改进；而超出覆盖范围，模型的误差又会误导规划器。称职驾驶的日志中很少包含能够扩大覆盖范围的失败案例，而通过交互学习的智能体只能通过自身犯错来获得这些失败经验，却未被展示应如何正确应对。我们提出SAFER框架，该框架离线学习他人所犯的、经专家评判的失败案例，并在线持续从自身驾驶中学习。离线阶段，部分训练的智能体和脚本化的可恢复扰动在仿真中构建失败场景，专家对每个访问到的状态标注其动作和价值，从而在规划器投入驾驶前，大部分探索范围已获覆盖。在规划阶段，分块潜世界模型快速预见后果以满足实时需求，基于风险感知的采样规划器通过“回报减去有界碰撞惩罚”来评估候选操作。在线阶段，一个闭环持续在驾驶偏离离线数据覆盖范围处精化模型。在与对比方法的闭环评估中，SAFER在CARLA Leaderboard路线上取得了最高的路线完成率和每步奖励，分别为0.95和0.77（基于特权鸟瞰视角），并在MetaDrive中取得了最高的回合奖励，常规交通下为53.4，安全关键交通下为49.0。项目页面：https://ys-qu.github.io/safer-website。

Decentralized Robot Swarm Human Swarm Demonstrations SwarmVLA Egocentric Observations Multi-Robot Collaboration

简要说明：EgoSwarm提出了一种从人类群体演示中学习去中心化多机器人协作的框架，利用群体交互包（SIP）和群体交互令牌（SIT）恢复跨智能体交互，并训练局部观测条件下的SwarmVLA策略，在协作任务中平均成功率72.2%。

TLDR: EgoSwarm introduces a framework for learning decentralized multi-robot collaboration from human swarm demonstrations, using Swarm Interaction Packets (SIP) and Tokens (SIT) to recover cross-agent interactions and a SwarmVLA policy for local observation-conditioned control, achieving 72.2% success on collaborative tasks.

Abstract: Advancing from individual embodied intelligence to swarm embodied intelligence is a key step toward scalable, general-purpose embodied intelligence. Complex tasks often require multiple agents to divide responsibilities, coordinate handovers, and manipulate shared objects collaboratively. However, existing robot learning methods rely primarily on individual demonstrations, which provide limited supervision for modeling dependencies among agent actions, while collecting multi-robot demonstrations at scale remains difficult. Human egocentric demonstrations offer a scalable alternative, yet distributed observations from multiple participants cannot directly supervise decentralized policies. We introduce EgoSwarm, the first robot learning framework for learning decentralized multi-robot collaboration from human swarm demonstrations. EgoSwarm collects more than 50 hours of collaborative experience using a robot-free multi-person egocentric system. We introduce the Swarm Interaction Packet (SIP) and Swarm Interaction Token (SIT) to recover cross-agent interaction relations from distributed observations and transform this experience into local supervision consistent with deployment-time information constraints. We further propose SwarmVLA, which transfers knowledge of swarm behavior to decentralized policies conditioned on local observations, local history, and limited neighbor messages. SwarmVLA achieves a mean success rate of 72.2% across three categories of multi-robot collaborative tasks. Project page:https://xitong-c.github.io/EgoSwarm_Page/.

ReactBench-AD: Do Driving VLAs and World-Action Models React to Imminent Hazards in Their Path? AutonomousDriving

counterfactual benchmark hazard reaction vision-language-action models world-action models fine-tuning

简要说明：ReactBench-AD 通过在真实驾驶场景中插入反事实的迫近危险来测试驾驶 VLA 和世界动作模型是否会刹车，发现模型反应薄弱，但短暂微调可显著提升其对危险的反应能力。

TLDR: ReactBench-AD is a benchmark that inserts counterfactual imminent hazards into real driving scenes to test whether driving VLAs and World-Action Models react by braking, finding weak reactions but showing that short fine-tuning can substantially improve hazard response.

Abstract: Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs) and World-Action Models (WAMs) have emerged in autonomous driving with the promise of strong generalization to long-tail scenarios. Although these models achieve state-of-the-art results on autonomous driving benchmarks, most existing benchmarks are dominated by nominal scenarios and therefore cannot test this promise. To test one such long-tail capability that is critical for safety, we introduce ReactBench-AD, a benchmark that measures how driving models react to objects that appear in the ego vehicle's path. Built on real NAVSIM and PhysicalAI-Autonomous-Vehicles scenes, ReactBench-AD composites a crossing pedestrian, a standing pedestrian, or a stationary car consistently across all camera views and history frames. Each edited scene thus forms an exact, pixel-level counterfactual pair with its original, which allows us to isolate the causal effect of the inserted object on the predicted trajectory. Evaluating four VLAs and two WAMs, we find that they react weakly, often barely at all. Finally, we test how easily one of the evaluated VLAs, ReCogDrive, can be taught to brake hard for imminent hazards: a short fine-tuning stage on edited scenes substantially improves its reactions, with the learned behavior generalizing to unseen objects and fully real scenes, at minimal cost to nominal driving. These results call into question the out-of-the-box long-tail capabilities of these models, but suggest that they can be a strong foundation for acquiring them with little additional training, highlighting the value of real or synthetic data that demonstrates the long-tail behaviors nominal datasets rarely contain. Code and data will be made publicly available.

PlanDiv: Test-Time Scaling with Latent Plans for Vision-Language-Action Models VLA

test-time scaling latent plans masked-diffusion executor action candidates VLA policies

简要说明：PlanDiv 通过引入一种动作头来提出多样化的潜在计划假设，使计划条件下的动作候选优于基于噪声的重采样，在 CALVIN、LIBERO 和真实机器人任务上均取得更好表现。

TLDR: PlanDiv improves test-time scaling for VLA models by introducing an action head that proposes diverse latent plan hypotheses, enabling plan-conditioned action candidates that outperform noise-based resampling across CALVIN, LIBERO, and real-robot tasks.

Abstract: Test-time scaling improves vision-language-action (VLA) policies by drawing several action candidates and executing the best one. We find that its returns diminish quickly when the candidates are resampled from one decoder: they differ by noise rather than by plan, and for each of three resampling sources, going from four to eight candidates adds only 0.06-0.07 to the average number of completed tasks on CALVIN. We introduce PlanDiv, an action head that makes the candidates differ by plan: a lightweight planner proposes K latent plan hypotheses, a shared masked-diffusion executor decodes one action chunk per plan from a single backbone pass, and the policy executes the chunk that its own likelihood ranks highest. The plans are learned from demonstrations alone, without plan labels or rewards. With the backbone, data and training recipe held fixed, eight plan-conditioned candidates raise five-task chain success on CALVIN ABC$\rightarrow$D from 56.9% to 69.9%, while eight candidates from autoregressive, plan-free or same-plan resampling reach at most 64.9%. The same-plan baseline is PlanDiv's own checkpoint with the plan held fixed, so this gap comes from the plans, not from drawing more samples.
