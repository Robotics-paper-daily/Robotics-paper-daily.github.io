Cairn: Object-Centric 3D Trajectory Priors for Steering Frozen VLAs VLA

object-centric 3D trajectory priors frozen VLA hierarchical planning sim-to-real LIBERO

简要说明：Cairn 引入以物体为中心的 3D 轨迹先验，在测试时引导冻结的视觉-语言-动作模型（VLA），在无需微调的情况下提高新布局中的操作成功率，并保留已有技能。

TLDR: Cairn introduces object-centric 3D trajectory priors that steer frozen vision-language-action models (VLAs) at test time, improving manipulation success in novel layouts without fine-tuning and preserving existing skills.

Abstract: Vision-language-action models (VLAs) are capable low-level controllers, but they tie where objects go to the layouts they were trained on: when objects are moved or obstacles block the way, they carry them to the wrong place or into collisions. Fine-tuning on new layouts needs robot data for each one and erodes skills the model already has. We introduce Cairn, which keeps the VLA frozen and steers it at test time along learned 3D trajectories of the object in its gripper. A small prior reads point clouds of the scene and the held object, along with its pose when grasped and the instruction, and samples SE(3) trajectories up to its release that lead it around obstacles and turn it to fit through gaps. The prior and the VLA form the two levels of a hierarchical planner in which feedback runs both ways: the VLA's intended motion chooses and reshapes the trajectories, and the chosen one guides the VLA's denoising. Its predicted actions, mapped to the held object's poses through the grasp, are pulled toward the next waypoint, while the VLA's weights stay untouched. Across 1,941 held-out LIBERO scenes with displaced objects and obstacles, Cairn raises the success rate of a frozen π0.5 from 24.5% to 95.1%, above that of a π0.5 fine-tuned on our training layouts, without hurting standard LIBERO. On the third-party LIBERO-PRO benchmark, it raises success from 27.3% to 38.0%, and from 54.4% to 81.1% on the episodes it steers. On a real Franka, with a prior trained purely in simulation, it guides the frozen DROID π0.5 around an obstacle and to a placement at the limit of the arm's reach, where it otherwise fails. All code, models and data will be made public.

摘要（中文）：视觉-语言-动作模型（VLA）是能力较强的低级控制器，但它们将物体的去向绑定于训练时所见的布局：当物体被移动或障碍物阻挡路径时，它们会将物体带到错误的位置或导致碰撞。针对新布局的微调需要为每种布局采集机器人数据，并会削弱模型已有的技能。我们提出Cairn，该方法保持VLA冻结不变，并在测试时沿学习到的夹爪中物体的3D轨迹对其进行引导。一个小型先验模型读取场景点云、被抓持物体点云、抓取时的物体位姿以及指令，并采样从其释放前的SE(3)轨迹，使其绕过障碍物并转动以穿过狭窄间隙。先验模型与VLA构成分层规划器的两个层级，其间反馈双向流动：VLA的预期动作选择并重塑轨迹，而被选中的轨迹则引导VLA的去噪过程。VLA预测的动作通过抓取关系映射到被抓持物体的位姿，被拉向下一路点，而VLA的权重保持不变。在1,941个物体被移位且存在障碍物的保留LIBERO场景中，Cairn将冻结的π0.5的成功率从24.5%提升至95.1%，超过了在训练布局上微调的π0.5，同时不影响标准LIBERO的性能。在第三方LIBERO-PRO基准上，它将成功率从27.3%提升至38.0%，而在其实际引导的片段中，成功率从54.4%提升至81.1%。在真实Franka机械臂上，使用纯仿真训练的先验模型，它引导冻结的DROID π0.5绕过障碍物并放置到机械臂可达范围的极限位置——而该模型在无引导时无法完成此任务。所有代码、模型和数据将公开。

FineDex: Bridging Fine-Grained Human-Object Interaction Generation to Dexterous Humanoid Manipulation HumanoidEmbodied

fine-grained HOI generation dexterous manipulation humanoid robot physics-based digital twin finger-level control

简要说明：FineDex 将细粒度的人-物交互生成与灵巧人形操作相连接，使手指级别的控制指令能够通过基于物理的练习和真实机器人部署得以表达和执行。

TLDR: FineDex bridges fine-grained human-object interaction generation with dexterous humanoid manipulation, enabling finger-level control instructions to be expressed and executed via physics-based practice and real-robot deployment.

Abstract: Dexterity is, at its core, finger-level control, yet existing dexterous manipulation and human-object interaction (HOI) generation methods rarely let one specify how an interaction is performed: five-fingered hands are often reduced to grippers, or finger usage is copied from individual human demonstrations. We present FineDex, a framework that makes finger-level intent both expressible and executable. FineDex learns coordinated human body, hand, and object motions that follow fine-grained instructions on hand selection, individual fingers, and contacts with articulated objects. To bridge generation and execution, FineDex leverages generated interactions as actionable priors for an agent that discovers physically feasible contact strategies through practice in a physics-based digital twin. By adapting at test time without per-instance retraining, FineDex generalizes across fine-grained instructions and object configurations. Experiments demonstrate state-of-the-art HOI generation quality and fine-grained controllability, substantial improvements in dexterous manipulation success, and successful deployment on a real dexterous humanoid robot.

摘要（中文）：灵巧性的核心在于手指级控制，然而现有的灵巧操作与人体-物体交互（HOI）生成方法很少允许用户指定交互的具体执行方式：五指手常被简化为夹爪，或者手指的使用方式仅从单个人体示范中复制而来。我们提出了FineDex，一个使手指级意图既可表达又可执行的框架。FineDex学习协调的人体、手部和物体运动，能够遵循关于手的选择、单个手指以及与可动关节物体接触的细粒度指令。为了桥接生成与执行，FineDex将生成的交互作为可操作先验，供智能体在基于物理的数字孪生中通过实践发现物理上可行的接触策略。通过测试时自适应而无需逐实例重新训练，FineDex在细粒度指令和物体配置上均展现出良好的泛化能力。实验结果表明，FineDex在HOI生成质量和细粒度可控性方面达到了当前最优水平，在灵巧操作成功率上取得了显著提升，并成功部署到了真实灵巧人形机器人上。

DexLink: Synthesizing Dexterous Behaviors from Chained Hand-object Poses Manipulation

dexterous manipulation hand-object interaction residual reinforcement learning sim-to-real transfer retargeting

简要说明：DexLink通过链式手-物交互模板合成多样化灵巧操作策略，利用优化重定向和残差强化学习将参考轨迹优化为闭环策略，实现了强大的仿真到现实迁移。

TLDR: DexLink synthesizes diverse dexterous manipulation policies by chaining hand-object interaction templates, using optimization-based retargeting and residual reinforcement learning to refine reference trajectories into closed-loop policies, achieving strong simulation-to-real transfer.

Abstract: Synthesizing diverse dexterous manipulation policies is challenging: existing simulation-based approaches rely on human demonstrations which are difficult to scale, or use generic reward functions, which provide limited control over the hand-object interactions. We propose DexLink, which addresses these limitations by synthesizing diverse dexterous behaviors from sparse, reusable hand-object interaction templates. Given key task waypoints, DexLink retargets static interaction templates to target objects using an optimization that preserves spatial hand-object relationships. It then links the retargeted poses into an approximate reference trajectory and uses residual reinforcement learning to refine this reference into a closed-loop policy in simulation. Experiments spanning single-hand, bimanual, articulated, and dynamic tasks demonstrate that DexLink provides fine-grained control over task timing, grasp types, and high-level execution. The modular interaction templates transfer reliably across object scales, instances, and categories; without object-specific human demonstrations, the framework also matches the performance of dense reference-tracking methods. Finally, zero-shot deployment of policies trained purely in simulation shows promise for the simulation-based framework.

摘要（中文）：合成多样化的灵巧操作策略具有挑战性：现有基于仿真实验的方法依赖难以规模化的人类示范，或使用通用奖励函数，从而对手部-物体交互的控制能力有限。我们提出DexLink，通过从稀疏、可复用的手部-物体交互模板中合成多样化灵巧行为来解决这些局限。给定关键任务路径点，DexLink利用保持空间手部-物体关系的优化方法，将静态交互模板重定向至目标物体。随后，它将重定向后的姿态串联为近似的参考轨迹，并采用残差强化学习将该参考轨迹优化为仿真环境中的闭环策略。涵盖单手、双手、关节式及动态任务的实验表明，DexLink能够对任务时序、抓取类型和高层执行提供细粒度控制。模块化交互模板可跨物体尺度、实例和类别可靠迁移；在无需针对特定物体人类示范的情况下，该框架的性能亦能达到密集参考跟踪方法的水准。最后，纯仿真训练策略的零样本部署展示了基于仿真框架的应用潜力。

MERLoc: Robust Robot Localization in Dynamic Environments with Pose-Based Retrieval over 3D Gaussian Splatting References Perception3D

robot localization 3D Gaussian Splatting pose-based retrieval dynamic environments onboard navigation

简要说明：MERLoc 利用 3D 高斯溅射扫描构建密集功能参考记忆，并通过基于位姿的检索在动态环境中实现鲁棒机器人定位，优于基于外观的检索，并支持机载实时导航。

TLDR: MERLoc builds a dense functional reference memory from a 3D Gaussian Splatting scan and uses pose-based retrieval to robustly localize robots in dynamic environments, outperforming appearance-based retrieval and enabling real-time onboard navigation.

Abstract: Robots operating in previously mapped spaces must localize accurately despite moving people and rearranged objects. Most localization pipelines match the query against a sparse set of captured reference images retrieved by appearance, which is brittle under scene change and repeating structures. We present MERLoc, which turns a single metric 3D Gaussian Splatting scan into a dense Functional Reference Memory. Reference views are sampled over the navigable space for the robot's morphology and sensors at a target density and rendered offline. Pre-encoding them for the chosen off-the-shelf localizer removes the reference encoder from inference. A pose-based retrieval strategy then selects references by spatial proximity and viewing direction rather than appearance, sidestepping visual ambiguities whenever a prior exists. Across one classical and four learned localizers in simulated and real dynamic scenes, pose-based retrieval matches or outperforms appearance retrieval in most cases, even when the latter is gated by the same prior. On real data, it lowers position RMSE by 15-60 % in nine of ten backend/split combinations, and the rendered memory outperforms real-image caches posed by Structure-from-Motion. The system runs onboard a Jetson Orin NX at 5 Hz, enabling a navigation policy to successfully traverse a hallway with people roaming and rearranging objects.

摘要（中文）：在先前已测绘的空间中运行的机器人，必须在有行人移动和物体被重新布置的情况下，仍能实现精确定位。大多数定位流程会将查询图像与一组由外观检索得到的稀疏参考图像进行匹配，这种方法在场景变化和重复结构面前较为脆弱。我们提出了MERLoc，它将一次度量三维高斯泼溅扫描转换为一组密集的功能参考记忆。参考视图根据机器人的形态和传感器，在可导航空间中按目标密度进行采样，并离线渲染。对这些视图进行预编码后，所选用的现成定位器无需在推理阶段运行参考编码器。随后，一种基于姿态的检索策略根据空间邻近性和视角方向而非外观来选择参考视图，从而在存在先验信息时规避视觉歧义。在模拟和真实动态场景中，我们对一个经典定位器和四个学习型定位器进行了实验，结果表明，在大多数情况下，基于姿态的检索性能与外观检索相当或更优，即使后者也受到相同先验信息约束时也是如此。在真实数据上，该方法在十种后端/分割组合中的九种情况下将位置均方根误差降低了15%至60%，且渲染记忆的性能优于由运动恢复结构（Structure-from-Motion）生成的真实图像缓存。该系统可在Jetson Orin NX上以5 Hz频率运行，使导航策略能够成功穿越一条有行人走动和物体被重新布置的走廊。

RePace: Reasoning about Demonstration Speedup for Faster Vision-Language-Action Policies VLA

vision-language-action model demonstration speedup data augmentation pretrained language model robotic manipulation

简要说明：RePace 是一个离线推理框架，通过预训练语言模型为人类演示选择每段加速因子，从而加快 VLA 策略训练，在 LIBERO 和 RoboCasa-Kitchen 上提升了任务效率。

TLDR: RePace is an offline reasoning framework that accelerates VLA policy training by using a pretrained language model to select per-segment speedup factors for human demonstrations, improving task efficiency on LIBERO and RoboCasa-Kitchen.

Abstract: Since vision-language-action (VLA) policies can inherit the slow pace of human demonstrations, accelerating these demonstrations before fine-tuning can lead to faster VLA policies without an additional speed-selection module at deployment. Several offline speedup methods have focused on identifying segments that require less precision and assigning predefined speedup factors to the segments. However, even within a single segment, a speedup factor suitable for steady motion may be too high when precise alignment is required. To this end, this paper presents RePace, an offline reasoning framework that determines how much to accelerate each part of a demonstration. For the same recorded movement, RePace measures how much each candidate speedup factor shortens the sequence and compares the resulting observation-action pairs used for training. Given the task instruction, a pretrained language model uses the visual context and original motion of successive windows to interpret these candidate retiming effects and select a speedup profile for the complete episode. Experimental results demonstrate that RePace outperforms the baselines in overall task execution efficiency on both LIBERO and RoboCasa-Kitchen. On all three tasks with a gripper-equipped Franka Research 3 arm, RePace matches or exceeds the acceleration baselines in success rate and completes successful trials faster.

摘要（中文）：由于视觉-语言-动作（VLA）策略可能继承人类演示的缓慢节奏，在微调前加速这些演示可以在部署时无需额外的速度选择模块即可获得更快的VLA策略。已有的离线加速方法主要侧重于识别需要较低精度的片段，并为这些片段分配预设的加速因子。然而，即使在同一片段内，当需要精确对齐时，适用于平稳运动的加速因子可能过高。为此，本文提出了RePace，一种离线推理框架，用于确定演示中每个部分应加速多少。对于相同的记录动作，RePace衡量每个候选加速因子缩短序列的程度，并比较由此产生的用于训练的状态-动作对。在给定任务指令的情况下，预训练语言模型利用连续窗口的视觉上下文和原始运动来解读这些候选重定时效果，并为完整回合选择加速曲线。实验结果表明，RePace在LIBERO和RoboCasa-Kitchen上的整体任务执行效率均优于基线方法。在配备夹爪的Franka Research 3机械臂上执行的所有三项任务中，RePace在成功率上达到或超过加速基线，且成功试验的完成速度更快。

Memory Is Not Ground Truth: Revising Navigation Authority under Unreliable VLM Evidence for Persistent Semantic Navigation Other

persistent semantic navigation vision-language model memory update field robot false-negative verification

简要说明：本文研究了野外机器人持续语义导航中VLM证据不可靠的问题，提出一种依赖感知、可逆的记忆更新机制，修改记忆的可操作性而非将检测失败视为目标缺失，并在波士顿动力Spot上进行了验证。

TLDR: This paper investigates unreliable VLM evidence in persistent semantic navigation for field robots, proposing a dependence-aware, reversible memory-update mechanism that revises memory actionability rather than treating failed detection as absence, validated on a Boston Dynamics Spot.

Abstract: Vision-language models (VLMs) enable robots to reason about semantic and spatio-temporal context, verify task-relevant objects, and guide sensing and navigation. Persistent memory can reduce redundant search for field robots, but when should a memory continue to guide behavior? Repeated misses may suggest that a memory is stale. Our experiments show why this evidence can remain ambiguous under VLM-based perception. Using a Boston Dynamics Spot with four fixed cameras and a persistent spatio-temporal visual memory, we measure negative-verification behavior across targets, viewpoints, language queries, and repeated revisits. False-negative rates vary substantially across objects, nearby revisits often reproduce the same detector outcome, and some matched target-present and target-removed experiments yield identical miss histories. This ambiguity persists under changed observation geometry, identity-preserving query reformulation, and conditioning on a previously verified target image. These findings raise two questions: is the object still there, and is this memory still useful for navigation? We introduce a dependence-aware, reversible memory-update mechanism that avoids double-counting correlated misses and stops using unsupported memories as direct navigation goals without claiming that the object is absent. Later positive evidence can restore a memory's use for navigation. One resident onboard VLM is shared across memory verification, semantic frontier reasoning, and in-motion perception. In three matched real-robot decision contexts, an episode-local baseline selected a stale memory as its navigation objective in every case, whereas our method selected semantic frontiers in all three. A complementary trial re-verified the target during motion and promoted fresh memory. These results support revising memory actionability rather than interpreting failed detection as object absence.

Not all world models can doubt: how training objective and architecture shape epistemic uncertainty in JEPA and RSSM world models WorldModel

epistemic uncertainty world models JEPA RSSM model-based control

简要说明：本文实证比较了JEPA和RSSM世界模型中的认知不确定性估计，发现RSSM因其循环平均和重建目标会衰减状态扰动，而JEPA将变化直接传递到不确定性估计，影响未知状态检测和规划等下游任务。

TLDR: This paper empirically compares epistemic uncertainty estimation in JEPA and RSSM world models, showing that RSSMs attenuate state perturbations due to their recurrent averaging and reconstruction objective, while JEPAs propagate changes more directly to uncertainty estimates, affecting downstream tasks like unfamiliar-state detection and planning.

Abstract: World models predict how an environment responds to actions, so that candidate actions can be evaluated before execution. Executed actions, or decisions, taken in this manner are only as reliable as the model's predictive capabilities, and predictions in turn are only reliable within the model's experience. Epistemic uncertainty (EU) is the signal that marks where that experience ends. In this work we offer an extensive empirical study on how training objectives and architectures shape epistemic uncertainty of world models. More precisely, we present a controlled comparison of post-hoc EU (last-layer Laplace and ensembles) between two dominant models of different paradigms: 1) LeWorldModel (LeWM): A joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA), which predicts the next embedding without a decoder, and 2) DreamerV3: A recurrent state-space model (RSSM), which reconstructs the observation from a recurrent latent state. In ten simulated visual-control environments, we follow a controlled state perturbation of the environment state through every stage of the models up to the final EU estimate. We find that the RSSM attenuates the state perturbation through two mechanisms before it reaches an estimator on the predictor feature. Its recurrent update averages over context, so a change in a single frame reaches the state at a fraction of its size and recovers only as the change persists. Its reconstruction objective weights an entity by the pixels it covers, so a small object can be lost due to the training objective. The JEPA has neither mechanism and passes the change through all stages to the uncertainty. We further show that this translates to gaps in five uncertainty related downstream tasks, from unfamiliar-state detection to uncertainty-penalised planning: the RSSM uncertainty misses deviations that its own posterior registers, whereas the JEPA uncertainty reports what its model sees.
